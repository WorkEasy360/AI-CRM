## Overview
Every deal has a quick **Deal Details** panel on the board and a full deal page with tabs for everything linked to it.

## Steps
1. Click **Pipeline** in the left sidebar.
2. On the **Kanban** view, click the deal's name on its card.
3. Review the **Deal Details** panel: stage, **Deal Value**, **Expected Close**, **Owner**, **Source** and **Product**, plus the **Activities**, **Notes** and **Files** tabs.
4. Click **Open full deal** at the bottom of the panel for the full page.

## Result
The full deal page shows the amount, status, owner and close date at the top, a clickable stage track, metric tiles (**Value**, **Stage**, **Probability**, **Expected close**, **AI score**, **Risk**) and tabs: **Overview**, **Timeline**, **Contacts**, **Products**, **Connected**, **Activities**, **Notes** and **Files**. Some tabs, such as **Communication** and **AI Insights**, appear only if your role allows them.

## Tips
- In the **List** view, clicking a row opens the full deal page directly.
- Click **Back to pipeline** at the top of the deal page to return to the board.
- The left column of the deal page shows the related contact and company, tags, description and key dates.

## Troubleshooting
- **The deal cannot be found.** It may have been deleted, or it is outside what your role can see.
