## Overview
Update a person's name, email, phone, company, status and other details whenever they change.

## Before you start
- Sales Representatives can edit contacts they own. Owner, Admin and Sales Manager can edit all contacts. Viewers cannot edit.

## Steps
1. Click **Contacts** in the left sidebar, then **People (Contacts)**.
2. Click the person's name to open the contact.
3. Click **Edit** at the top right.
4. Change **First name**, **Last name**, **Email**, **Phone**, **Company** or **Status** as needed.
5. Click **More details** to change **Job title**, **Source**, **Description**, WhatsApp consent, address or custom fields.
6. Click **Save changes**.

## Result
A "Contact updated" message appears and the contact page shows the new details.

## Tips
- You can also edit from the **Individuals** tab: click the ⋮ button on the row and choose **Edit**.
- A contact needs at least a first name, last name or email.
- Switch on **Contact agreed to receive WhatsApp messages** only when the person has given consent; template messages need it.

## Troubleshooting
- **"This record was changed by someone else"?** Click **Reload** in the form, check the latest values, then save again.
- **No Edit button?** You do not own this contact or your role cannot edit contacts.
