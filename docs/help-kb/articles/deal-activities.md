## Overview
Activities are the calls, tasks and meetings linked to a deal. Keeping a next activity on every open deal stops it from stalling; cards without one show **No next step**.

## Before you start
- You need `activities.create`. Owner, Admin, Sales Manager and Sales Representative have it.

## Steps
1. Click **Pipeline** in the left sidebar.
2. Click the deal's name on its card to open the **Deal Details** panel.
3. Under the tabs, click a quick action: **Call** to log a call, **Task** or **Meeting** to schedule one.
4. Fill in the activity form and save it.

## Result
The activity is linked to the deal and appears on the panel's **Activities** tab, newest first. Completed ones show a green check. The card shows the next activity instead of **No next step**.

## Tips
- On the full deal page, the **Activities** tab switches between **Open** and **Completed** activities and has **Task**, **Call** and **Meeting** buttons.
- The quick actions also include **Email**, **WhatsApp** and **Note** when your role and the contact allow them.

## Troubleshooting
- **No quick action buttons.** Your role cannot create activities (for example, Viewer).
