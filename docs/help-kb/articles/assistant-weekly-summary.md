## Overview
The top of the AI Assistant page summarises your week and lists today's follow-ups, so you can act before you ask anything. The figures come straight from the CRM.

## Steps
1. Click **AI Assistant** in the left sidebar.
2. Read **Here's your CRM summary for this week**: **New Leads**, **Deals**, **Revenue** and **Pending Follow-ups**. Badges compare with the 7 days before.
3. In **Your Follow-up Tasks (Today)**, review today's timed calls, tasks and meetings.
4. Tick the checkbox next to an item to mark it as done.
5. Click the button on the right of an item (**Call**, **Meeting** or **WhatsApp**) to open it in Activities.
6. Read the **AI Suggestion** line, if shown, and click **View** (or **View Leads**) to open the related list.
7. On the right, open any line under **AI Insights & Recommendations**.

## Result
Completed follow-ups are marked done, and you can jump to any record that needs attention.

## Tips
- The summary figures need Dashboard access, and the follow-up list needs Activities access.

## Troubleshooting
- **"Nothing scheduled for today."** You have no timed activities today.
- **"Nothing needs your attention right now."** There are no insights to show.
