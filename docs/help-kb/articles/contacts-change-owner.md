## Overview
The owner is the team member responsible for a contact or company. New records are owned by the person who creates them.

## Before you start
- Only members who can edit all records may change the owner: Owner, Admin and Sales Manager. Sales Representatives do not see the Owner field.

## Steps
1. Click **Contacts** in the left sidebar, then **People (Contacts)**.
2. Click the person's name to open the contact.
3. Click **Edit**.
4. Click **More details**.
5. In **Owner**, choose the team member.
6. Click **Save changes**.

## Result
A "Contact updated" message appears and the profile card shows "Owner:" with the new name.

## Tips
- For a company, open it from **Contacts** → **Companies**, click **Edit**, then **More details** → **Owner**.
- To move many records at once, select them in the list and use **Reassign**.
- Records without an owner show "Unassigned".
