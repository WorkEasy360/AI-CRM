## Overview
If you are a member of more than one organization, you can move between them from the Account menu. Each organization's data is kept completely separate.

## Before you start
- The **Organization** section only appears when you have more than one active membership, for example after accepting an invitation from a second company.

## Steps
1. Click your avatar at the right end of the top bar to open the **Account menu**.
2. Find the **Organization** section. The workspace you are in now is selected.
3. Click the name of the organization you want to open.

## Result
The CRM reloads in the chosen organization and opens the **Pipeline** page. The top of the Account menu now shows that organization and your role in it.

## Tips
- Your role can differ between organizations, so menus and buttons may change after you switch.
- You can also switch from **Settings → Workspace → Organization Details**: in the **Your organizations** card, click **Open** next to an organization. The same card has **New organization** to create another workspace.

## Troubleshooting
- **I do not see an Organization section.** You belong to only one organization, or your other membership is not active yet.
- **"Could not switch organization."** Try again. If it keeps failing, your membership may have been disabled; contact that organization's administrator.
