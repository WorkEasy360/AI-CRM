## Overview
The AI assistant only works with CRM data the person asking can already see. Owners and Admins decide whether generative AI is allowed at all.

## Before you start
- Only the Owner and Admins can change the AI policy.

## Steps
1. Click **Settings** in the left sidebar.
2. In the **AI Assistant** card, click **AI Settings**.
3. In the **AI policy** card, turn the **Generative AI** switch on or off.

## Result
- **Off**: no customer text leaves your workspace. Ask WorkEasy keeps working and answers from your CRM records and past conversations, without written analysis or drafts.
- **On**: to write an answer or a draft, the assistant sends the relevant parts of records the member can already see to an external AI model. It never sends passwords, keys or other workspaces' data.

## Tips
- The assistant only writes drafts. A person reviews and sends every email or WhatsApp message, and messages sent from a draft are marked "AI-assisted".
- Lead scores and deal risk are rules-based calculations, not AI predictions.
- Set a **Monthly budget** in the same card. When it is reached, Ask WorkEasy switches to CRM answers for the rest of the month.
- The **How AI is used** card on the same page summarises these rules.

## Troubleshooting
- **"You have reached the AI request limit for this hour."** Each person has an hourly limit. Try again a little later.
- **"Your workspace has used its AI budget for today."** An administrator can review usage on the **AI Settings** page.
