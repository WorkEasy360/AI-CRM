## Overview
The role hierarchy is your reporting structure. A person sees records owned by anyone below them when their profile grants **Own + subordinates** access.

## Before you start
- The Owner and Admins can change the hierarchy.
- The hierarchy only matters for profiles that use **Own + subordinates**. Set that up on the **Profiles** tab.

## Steps
1. Click **Settings** in the left sidebar.
2. In the **Workspace** card, click **Roles & Permissions**.
3. Click the **Role hierarchy** tab.
4. In the **Hierarchy** card, type a name under **New role**, for example "Regional manager".
5. In the drop-down next to it, choose **Top of hierarchy** or **Reports to** an existing role.
6. Click **Add**.
7. In the **People** card, choose each person's place in the drop-down next to their name.

## Result
The hierarchy shows as an indented tree with the number of people at each level. Changes to a person's place save immediately.

## Tips
- Turn on **Peers** for a level to let people at the same level share each other's data.
- Choose **Not placed** to take someone out of the hierarchy.
- To delete a level, click the bin icon next to it.

## Troubleshooting
- **A manager still cannot see their reports' records.** Check that the manager's profile grants **Own + subordinates** (or more) for that module.
