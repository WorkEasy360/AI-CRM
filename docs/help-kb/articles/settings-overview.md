## Overview
The **Settings** page brings every administrative option together in six cards, so you can set up your workspace, team, communication channels, AI, security and integrations from one place.

## Before you start
- Every member can open Settings, but you only see the cards and links your role allows. Owners and Admins see almost everything; other roles see fewer items.

## Steps
1. Click **Settings** at the bottom of the left sidebar.
2. Find the card for the area you want to change:
   - **Workspace**: **Organization Details**, **Users & Teams**, **Roles & Permissions**
   - **CRM Configuration**: **Pipelines & Stages**, **Custom Fields**, **Tags**
   - **Communication**: **Email Settings**, **WhatsApp Integration**, **Message Templates**, **Notifications**
   - **AI Assistant**: **AI Settings**, **Usage & Limits**
   - **Security & Data**: **Password & 2FA**, **Import & Export Data**, **Activity / Audit Log**
   - **Integrations & API**: **Integrations (Connected Apps)**, **API Keys**, **Webhooks**
3. Click the item you want to open.

## Result
The settings page opens. A settings menu on the left lets you jump between sections, and **Back to CRM** returns you to the pipeline.

## Tips
- Below the cards, **Also in Settings:** links to **Automation**, **Billing & Subscription** and **Price books**.
- You can also open Settings from the **Help** menu or the **Account menu** in the top bar.
- On a phone, use the **Settings section** drop-down at the top of each settings page to move between sections.

## Troubleshooting
- **A card or link I need is missing.** Your role does not include that permission. Ask an Owner or Admin to change your role.
