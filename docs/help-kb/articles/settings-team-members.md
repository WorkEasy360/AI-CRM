## Overview
Add people to a team so they share team-level visibility of records. A person can belong to several teams.

## Before you start
- Only the Owner and Admins can change team membership. Other members can only view who is in a team.
- The team must already exist.

## Steps
1. Click **Settings** in the left sidebar.
2. In the **Workspace** card, click **Users & Teams**.
3. Click the **Teams** tab.
4. On the team's card, click **Members**.
5. Under **Add a member**, click **Choose a member** and pick a person.
6. Click **Add**.
7. Repeat for each person, then click **Done**.

## Result
The person appears in the team's member list straight away, and the member count on the team card updates.

## Tips
- To take someone out of the team, click **Remove** next to their name in the same window.
- You can also set all of one person's teams at once: on the **Users** tab, open the **…** menu on their row, click **Assign team**, tick the teams and click **Save teams**.
- A person can belong to at most 20 teams.

## Troubleshooting
- **The list says "Everyone is already in this team".** All active members are already in it.
- **Assign teams says "No teams yet".** Create a team on the **Teams** tab first.
