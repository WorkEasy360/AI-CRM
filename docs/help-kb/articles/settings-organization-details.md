## Overview
Organization Details holds your workspace basics: the company name, the name shown at the top of the sidebar, the base currency, the time zone and the date format used across the CRM.

## Before you start
- Only the Owner and Admins can change these settings. Other members see the page read-only.

## Steps
1. Click **Settings** in the left sidebar.
2. In the **Workspace** card, click **Organization Details**.
3. In the **General** card, edit **Company name**.
4. Optionally enter a **CRM name** (shown at the top of the sidebar, up to 60 characters).
5. Choose a **Base currency**.
6. Choose a **Time zone**.
7. Choose a **Date format**.
8. Click **Save changes**.
9. If a **Confirm it's you** window appears, enter your password and click **Confirm**.

## Result
A message says **Organization updated**, and the new name, currency, time zone and date format apply for everyone in the workspace.

## Tips
- Click **Reset** to undo your unsaved edits.
- The **General** card also shows your current plan and the workspace status.

## Troubleshooting
- **The fields are greyed out.** The page shows "You can view these settings. Only owners and admins can change them." Ask the Owner or an Admin.
- **Save changes is disabled.** You have not changed anything yet.
