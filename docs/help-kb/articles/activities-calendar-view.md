## Overview
The **Calendar** tab shows your tasks, calls and meetings by month, week or day, including every occurrence of repeating meetings.

## Before you start
- You need `activities.view`. All built-in roles have it. A Sales Representative sees their own and their team's activities.

## Steps
1. Click **Activities** in the left sidebar.
2. Click the **Calendar** tab.
3. Choose **Month**, **Week** or **Day** at the top right.
4. Choose **My calendar** to see only your activities, or **Everyone** to see all activities you have access to.
5. Use the previous and next arrows to move through dates, or click **Today** to come back.
6. Click any event to open it.

## Result
The calendar shows each activity on its day. In week and day view, timed activities sit at their hour and all-day items appear in the **all day** row.

## Tips
- In month view, click **+N more** on a busy day, or click a day's date, to open that day.
- To schedule a meeting on a day, click the date of an empty day (month view) or double-click the day column (week or day view).
- Phones open the calendar in day view.
- You can also switch to the calendar from the table with the **Calendar view** button.

## Troubleshooting
- **"Could not load the calendar."** Click **Try again**.
