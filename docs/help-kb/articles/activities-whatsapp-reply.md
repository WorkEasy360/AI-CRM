## Overview
You send and reply to WhatsApp messages from a contact's **WhatsApp** tab. Free-text replies are allowed for 24 hours after the customer last wrote; outside that window you send an approved template.

## Before you start
- You need `whatsapp.send` (Owner, Admin, Sales Manager, Sales Representative).
- WhatsApp must be connected in **Settings** → **Communication** → **WhatsApp Integration**.
- The contact needs a phone number. Templates also need the contact's WhatsApp consent.

## Steps
1. Click **Contacts** in the left sidebar, then **People (Contacts)**.
2. Click the contact's name to open the record.
3. Click the **WhatsApp** tab.
4. If the window is open, type in **Write a reply…** and click **Send**.
5. If it shows **Window closed · templates only**, click **Send template message**.
6. Choose a **Template** and fill each **Value for {{1}}** box.
7. Click **Send template**.

## Result
"WhatsApp message queued" appears for template sends, and the message shows in the conversation. Ticks show **Sent**, **Delivered** and **Read** as the status updates.

## Tips
- Click **Templates and AI draft…** to open the full message box. If your role can use AI, pick a **Style** and click **Draft with AI**, then review the draft before sending.
- The **WhatsApp** quick action at the top of a contact page opens the same message box.

## Troubleshooting
- **"The 24-hour window has closed. Send an approved template instead."** Use **Send template message**.
- **"WhatsApp is not connected for this workspace"** Ask an Admin to connect it.
- **"No phone number"** Add a phone number to the contact.
