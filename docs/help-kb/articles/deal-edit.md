## Overview
You can change a deal's details from its full page, or make quick changes to the name, amount and close date straight from the board.

## Before you start
- You need `deals.update`. A Sales Representative can edit only their own deals; Sales Manager, Admin and Owner can edit all.

## Steps
1. Click **Pipeline** in the left sidebar.
2. Click the deal's name on its card, then click **Open full deal**.
3. Click **Edit** at the top right of the deal page.
4. In the **Edit Deal** panel, change the fields you need, such as **Amount**, **Expected close**, **Priority** or **Source**.
5. Click **Save changes**.

## Result
"Deal updated" appears and the deal page, board and list show the new values.

## Tips
- For a quick change on the board, click the **⋯** button on the card, choose **Edit details…**, change **Name**, **Amount** or **Expected close**, and click **Save**.
- In the **Deal Details** panel you can click the **Deal Value** to edit it, and change **Expected Close**, **Owner** and **Source** in place.
- The stage is not edited here. Move the deal to change its stage.
- If the deal has products, the amount is the total of its product lines and cannot be typed in.

## Troubleshooting
- **"Someone else changed this deal since you opened it."** Close the panel, reload the page and make your change again.
- **No Edit button.** You can only edit deals you own, or the deal is deleted.
