## Overview
If two-factor authentication is on for your account, the CRM asks for a 6-digit code after your password. This keeps your account safe even if someone learns your password.

## Before you start
- Keep your authenticator app, or your saved recovery codes, at hand.

## Steps
1. Enter your **Email** and **Password** and click **Sign in**.
2. On the **Two-factor authentication** screen, open your authenticator app.
3. Type the current 6-digit code into **Authentication code**. You can type one of your recovery codes instead.
4. Click **Verify**.

## Result
You are signed in and the CRM opens.

## Tips
- Authenticator codes change every few seconds. If a code is about to expire, wait for the next one.
- To sign in as someone else, click **Use a different account**.

## Troubleshooting
- **"That code was not accepted."** Check that your phone's clock is set automatically, then try a fresh code.
- **I lost my phone.** Use a recovery code. If you have none left, ask an administrator for help.
- **A banner says your organization requires two-factor authentication.** Click **Set it up now** in the banner to open the Security page and turn it on.
