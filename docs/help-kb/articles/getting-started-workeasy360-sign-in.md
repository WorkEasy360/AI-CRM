## Overview
If you already use other Workeasy360 products, you can open the CRM with the same Workeasy360 account instead of a separate CRM password.

## Before you start
- This option only appears when Workeasy360 sign-in is enabled for your installation. You then see the **Already a Workeasy360 user?** box on the **CRM Login** page.

## Steps
1. Open the **CRM Login** page.
2. In the **Already a Workeasy360 user?** box, click **Access WorkEasyCRM**.
3. If Workeasy360 asks, sign in there or confirm that you want to continue.

## Result
You return to the CRM signed in, on the page you were trying to open.

## Tips
- To use a CRM password instead, fill in the email and password form on the same page.
- With a Workeasy360 account, your password and two-step verification are managed in Workeasy360, not in the CRM.

## Troubleshooting
- **An error message appears on the CRM Login page after returning from Workeasy360.** Read the message, then click **Access WorkEasyCRM** again. If it repeats, contact your administrator.
