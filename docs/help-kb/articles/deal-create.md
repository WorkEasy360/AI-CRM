## Overview
A deal is a sales opportunity you track through a pipeline, from first contact to won or lost.

## Before you start
- You need `deals.create`. Owner, Admin, Sales Manager and Sales Representative have it; Viewer does not.
- At least one pipeline must exist.

## Steps
1. Click **Pipeline** in the left sidebar.
2. If you have several pipelines, select the pipeline the deal belongs to.
3. Click **Add Deal** at the right of the view tabs.
4. Type the **Deal name**.
5. In **Company**, type to search, or click **Create company** to add a new one.
6. In **Primary contact**, type to search, or click **Create contact** to add a new person.
7. Enter the **Amount** and check the **Currency**.
8. Set **Expected close**, **Priority** and **Source** if you know them.
9. Optionally add a **Description** and fill in **Additional Information**.
10. Click **Create deal**.

## Result
"Deal created" appears and the deal shows as a card in the first open stage of the selected pipeline. Its probability follows that stage.

## Tips
- If you can assign deals to others, choose the **Owner** at the top of the panel.
- Products are added on the deal after it is saved, on its **Products** tab.
- If you use another currency, a **Rate to** field asks for the exchange rate to your base currency.
- You can also start a deal from **Create** in the top bar, or from the **Deal** quick action on a contact or company.

## Troubleshooting
- **Add Deal is greyed out.** No pipeline exists yet. Ask a Sales Manager or Admin to create one.
- **"Deal name is required."** Enter a name before saving.
