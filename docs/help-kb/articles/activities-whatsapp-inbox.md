## Overview
The **WhatsApp** tab on the Activities page lists every WhatsApp message you can see, sent and received, newest first. It is a log; you reply from the contact's page.

## Before you start
- You need `whatsapp.view`. All built-in roles have it.
- Messages appear only after an Admin has connected WhatsApp in **Settings** → **Communication** → **WhatsApp Integration**.

## Steps
1. Click the green **WhatsApp inbox** button in the top bar, or click **Activities** in the left sidebar and then the **WhatsApp** tab.
2. Read the columns: **Activity** (message text or template name, with a sent or received arrow), **Related to**, **Date**, **Owner** and **Status**.
3. Click **Show more** at the bottom to load older messages.
4. To answer a message, click the contact's name in **Related to**.

## Result
The contact's page opens. Its **WhatsApp** tab shows the full conversation, where you can reply.

## Tips
- **Status** shows the delivery state, for example sent, delivered, read or failed.
- **Owner** shows who sent an outgoing message, or "Customer" for incoming ones.

## Troubleshooting
- **"No WhatsApp messages yet"** Nothing has been sent or received yet, or WhatsApp is not connected.
- **No WhatsApp tab?** Your role lacks `whatsapp.view`.
