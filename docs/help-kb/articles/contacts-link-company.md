## Overview
Linking a person to a company shows them on the company's **Contacts** tab and pre-fills the company on new deals and activities. A person can belong to one company; a company can have many people.

## Before you start
- You need to be able to edit the contact. Sales Representatives can edit contacts they own; Owner, Admin and Sales Manager can edit all.

## Steps
1. Click **Contacts** in the left sidebar, then **People (Contacts)**.
2. Click the person's name to open the contact.
3. Click **Edit**.
4. Open the **Company** menu and select the company.
5. Click **Save changes**.

## Result
A "Contact updated" message appears. The company shows on the contact's profile card and the person appears on the company's **Contacts** tab.

## Tips
- To link a new person while creating them, use **Company Name (Optional)** in the **Add Contact** panel. If the company does not exist yet, type its name and choose **Create company**.
- From a company page, open the **Contacts** tab and click **New contact**; the company is pre-selected.
- To unlink, choose **None** in the **Company** menu.
