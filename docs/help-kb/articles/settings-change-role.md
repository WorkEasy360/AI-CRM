## Overview
A member's role decides what they can see and do. Change it when someone's job changes, for example to make a Sales Representative a Sales Manager.

## Before you start
- Only the Owner and Admins can change roles.
- Admins can give the Admin, Sales Manager, Sales Representative and Viewer roles. Only the Owner can make someone an Owner or change another Owner's role.
- You cannot change your own role.

## Steps
1. Click **Settings** in the left sidebar.
2. In the **Workspace** card, click **Users & Teams**.
3. Find the person in the list.
4. Click the **…** actions button at the end of their row.
5. Click **Change role**.
6. Choose the new role under **Role**. A short description of the role appears below it.
7. Click **Save role**.
8. If a **Confirm it's you** window appears, enter your password and click **Confirm**.

## Result
A message says **Role updated** with the person's new role. They are signed out on every device and get the new permissions when they sign in again.

## Tips
- Custom profiles created under **Roles & Permissions** also appear in the **Role** list.
- To hand over the workspace, the Owner can give another member the Owner role.

## Troubleshooting
- **"The organization must keep at least one owner."** Make someone else Owner first.
- **There is no actions button on a row.** It is your own row, or the person is an Owner and you are not.
