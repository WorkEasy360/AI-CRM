## Overview
Notes keep the story of a person or company in one place: what was discussed, agreed or promised.

## Before you start
- Owner, Admin, Sales Manager and Sales Representative can add notes. Viewers can read notes but not add them.

## Steps
1. Click **Contacts** in the left sidebar, then **People (Contacts)**.
2. Click the person's name to open the contact.
3. Click the **Notes** tab.
4. Type in the box **Write a note… (plain text)**.
5. Click **Add note**.

## Result
The note appears at the top of the list with your name and the time, and is added to the **Timeline**.

## Tips
- For a quick note without leaving the Overview, click **Note** in the quick actions under the contact's details, type it, then click **Add note**.
- Use the pin icon (**Pin note**) to keep an important note at the top.
- You can edit or delete your own notes with the pencil and bin icons. Managers and Admins can change anyone's notes.
- Company pages have the same **Notes** tab.
