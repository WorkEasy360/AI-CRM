## Overview
Sign in with the email and password you used when you created your account or accepted an invitation.

## Before you start
- You need a verified account. If you have not verified your email yet, open the link in the verification email first.
- If the page shows **Already a Workeasy360 user?**, your installation also offers Workeasy360 sign-in. Click **Use another sign-in method** to show the password form.

## Steps
1. Open the **Sign in** page.
2. Enter your **Email**.
3. Enter your **Password**.
4. Click **Sign in**.
5. If asked for an **Authentication code**, enter the code from your authenticator app and click **Verify**.

## Result
The CRM opens on the **Pipeline** page, or on the page you were trying to open before you signed in.

## Tips
- Forgot your password? Click **Forgot password?** under the password field.
- No account yet? Click **Create account** at the bottom of the form.

## Troubleshooting
- **"Verify your email" appears.** Your account exists but the email is not verified. Open the verification link we sent you, click **Back to sign in**, and sign in again.
- **"Sign-in is not available right now. Contact your administrator."** Password sign-in is turned off for this installation. Ask your administrator how to sign in.
- **Sign-in failed.** Check that Caps Lock is off and the email is spelled correctly, or reset your password.
