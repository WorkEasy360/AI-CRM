## Overview
Log in with the email and password you used when you created your account or accepted an invitation.

## Before you start
- You need a verified account. If you have not verified your email yet, open the link in the verification email first.
- If the page also shows **Already a Workeasy360 user?**, your installation offers Workeasy360 sign-in as well. Either way of signing in works.

## Steps
1. Open the **CRM Login** page.
2. Enter your **Email Address**.
3. Enter your **Password**. Click the eye icon if you want to check what you typed.
4. On your own device, tick **Remember me** to stay signed in after closing the browser.
5. Click **Log In to CRM**.
6. If asked for an **Authentication code**, enter the code from your authenticator app and click **Verify**.

## Result
The CRM opens on the **Pipeline** page, or on the page you were trying to open before you logged in.

## Tips
- Without **Remember me**, you are signed out when the browser closes. Leave it off on a shared computer.
- Even with **Remember me**, you are asked to log in again after long inactivity.
- Forgot your password? Click **Forgot Password?** beside **Remember me**.
- No account yet? Click **Create CRM Account** under the **Log In to CRM** button.

## Troubleshooting
- **"Verify your email" appears.** Your account exists but the email is not verified. Open the verification link we sent you, click **Back to sign in**, and log in again.
- **"Sign-in is not available right now. Contact your administrator."** Password sign-in is turned off for this installation. Ask your administrator how to sign in.
- **Sign-in failed.** Check that Caps Lock is off and the email is spelled correctly, or reset your password.
