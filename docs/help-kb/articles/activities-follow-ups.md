## Overview
**Follow-ups** is a tab, not a separate activity type. It lists open activities that need a follow-up: calls logged with the outcome **Follow-up required**, and open tasks, calls or meetings whose title contains "follow" (for example "Follow up on quote").

## Before you start
- You need `activities.view` (all built-in roles).

## Steps
1. Click **Activities** in the left sidebar.
2. Click the **Follow-ups** tab.
3. Optional: narrow the list with **Search activities…**, the **Type**, **Status**, **Owner** and date range filters.
4. Click an item's title to open it.
5. When the follow-up is done, click the **⋮** actions button and choose **Mark complete**.

## Result
Completed follow-ups leave the tab. When none are left you see "No follow-ups waiting".

## Tips
- To add a follow-up, create a task with a title such as "Follow up with Priya", or log a call with the outcome **Follow-up required**.
- Follow-up activities created by automations also show here.
- The grouped list is not available on this tab.
