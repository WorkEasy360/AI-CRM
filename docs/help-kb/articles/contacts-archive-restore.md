## Overview
Contacts are not permanently deleted. Archiving hides a contact from lists and searches, and you can restore it at any time.

## Before you start
- Sales Representatives can archive contacts they own. Owner, Admin and Sales Manager can archive any contact.

## Steps
1. Click **Contacts** in the left sidebar, then **People (Contacts)**.
2. Click the person's name to open the contact.
3. Click the **More actions** button (⋯) next to **Edit**.
4. Click **Archive**.

## Result
An "Archived" message appears. The contact gets an **Archived** badge and no longer shows in the normal lists.

## Tips
- To restore: open the **Individuals** tab, change the view menu from **All contacts** to **Archived contacts**, click the ⋮ button on the row and choose **Restore**.
- You can also archive or restore from the ⋮ menu on any row in the Individuals tab.
- To archive several contacts at once, see bulk archive.

## Troubleshooting
- **No Archive option?** Your role cannot archive this contact, usually because someone else owns it.
