## Overview
Tags are short labels (such as "VIP" or "Hot lead") that you can put on people and companies to group and find them quickly.

## Before you start
- You need to be able to edit the record. Creating brand-new tags needs the tags.manage permission (Owner, Admin, Sales Manager).

## Steps
1. Click **Contacts** in the left sidebar, then **People (Contacts)**.
2. Click the person's name to open the contact.
3. In the **Tags** section on the left, click **Add tags** (or **Edit** if the record already has tags).
4. Click a tag to add it. A check mark shows it is applied.
5. Click a checked tag again to remove it.
6. Click outside the menu to close it.

## Result
The tags appear as coloured chips on the record and in the **Tags** column of All Contacts.

## Tips
- To list everyone with a tag, click **Tags** in the Contacts tab bar and pick the tag.
- When adding a contact in the **Add Contact** panel you can pick tags in the **Tags** box, or type a new one and press Enter if you may manage tags.
- Company pages have the same **Tags** section.

## Troubleshooting
- **"No tags defined yet."** Ask an Admin to create tags first.
