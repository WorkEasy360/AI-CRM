## Overview
The badge at the top right of the AI Assistant page shows how answers are produced. **Powered by AI** means the assistant writes full answers with analysis. **Knowledge search mode** means answers are assembled from your CRM records and conversations without AI-written analysis.

## Before you start
- Full AI answers need the AI copilot permission. Owner, Admin, Sales Manager and Sales Representative have it by default; Viewer does not.

## Steps
1. Click **AI Assistant** in the left sidebar.
2. Look at the badge at the top right of the page: **Powered by AI** or **Knowledge search mode**.
3. In the **Ask WorkEasy** panel, the **Knowledge search mode** badge appears next to the title when it applies.
4. Hover over the badge to read **Answers are assembled from your CRM records and conversations.**

## Result
You know whether to expect **Analysis** in answers. In knowledge search mode you still get facts from your CRM, related records and sources.

## Tips
- The badge also shows **Knowledge search mode** when generative AI is switched off for the workspace in **Settings → AI Assistant → AI Settings**.
- Even under **Powered by AI**, a single answer can arrive without analysis. A note under the answer then explains why, for example an hourly or monthly AI limit, or a plan without AI answers.

## Troubleshooting
- **I always see Knowledge search mode.** Ask an administrator to check your role's AI permission and the workspace AI settings.
- **"…The information above was retrieved directly from your CRM."** AI writing was not available for that answer. The facts shown are still correct; try again later.
