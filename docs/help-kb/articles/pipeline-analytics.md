## Overview
The Analytics tab shows how the selected pipeline performs: how many deals move through each stage, how many are won or lost, where deals come from and how each owner is doing.

## Steps
1. Click **Pipeline** in the left sidebar.
2. Click **Analytics** in the view tabs.
3. Choose a pipeline in the **Pipeline** drop-down.
4. Optionally narrow the data with **Search deals…**, the owner filter (**All Owners**), the source filter (**All Sources**) or the product filter (**All Products**).

## Result
Four panels appear:
- **Conversion by stage**: deals that reached each stage, as a share of those that entered the first.
- **Win / loss**: every closed deal in this pipeline.
- **Deals by source**: where deals came from, and how many were won.
- **Owner performance**: deals, wins and revenue won per owner.

## Tips
- Click **Clear** to remove the filters.
- The **Win Rate** tile above the tabs uses deals closed in the last 30 days.

## Troubleshooting
- **"No pipeline to analyse yet."** Create a pipeline first.
