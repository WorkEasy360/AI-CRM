## Overview
Most sign-in problems have one of four causes: a forgotten password, too many failed attempts, a lost authenticator app, or access removed by your administrator.

## Steps
1. On the **Two-factor authentication** screen, if you lost your phone, type one of your recovery codes in **Authentication code** instead of the 6-digit code.

## Result
You are signed in. Open **Password & 2FA** and set up your authenticator app again, then regenerate your recovery codes.

## Tips
- **Too many failed attempts.** After repeated wrong passwords, sign-in is blocked for a short while (up to about 15 minutes). Wait, then try again, or reset your password.
- **Forgot your password.** Use **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page.
- **Suspended or removed.** If your administrator suspended you, ask them to click **Reactivate**. If you were removed, ask them to invite you again.
- **Workeasy360 account.** Sign in with **Access WorkEasyCRM**; password and two-step problems are solved in Workeasy360.

## Troubleshooting
- **I lost my phone and have no recovery codes.** You cannot turn off two-factor authentication yourself without signing in. Contact support.
- **A banner says your organization requires two-factor authentication.** You are signed in, but must click **Set it up now** and set up an authenticator app before using the CRM.
