## Overview
New deals normally start in the first open stage. If a deal is already further along, add it straight into the right column from the Kanban board.

## Before you start
- You need `deals.create` (Owner, Admin, Sales Manager or Sales Representative).

## Steps
1. Click **Pipeline** in the left sidebar.
2. Make sure **Kanban View** is selected and the right pipeline is shown.
3. Scroll to the column of the stage you want.
4. Click **Add Deal** at the bottom of that column.
5. Fill in the **Create Deal** form, at least the **Deal name**.
6. Click **Create deal**.

## Result
"Deal created" appears and the card shows in the column you started from, with that stage's probability.

## Tips
- Each column's button reads "Add deal to" followed by the stage name for screen readers.

## Troubleshooting
- **No Add Deal button under the columns.** Your role cannot create deals (for example, Viewer).
