## Overview
The filter row above the Kanban board narrows the cards to the deals you care about. The summary tiles and the Analytics tab follow the same search, owner, source and product filters.

## Steps
1. Click **Pipeline** in the left sidebar.
2. Make sure **Kanban View** is selected.
3. Type part of a deal name in **Search deals…**.
4. Open the owner filter (**All Owners**) and choose **My deals** or a team member.
5. Open the source filter (**All Sources**) and choose **WhatsApp**, **Facebook Ads**, **Website**, **Referral** or **Other**.
6. Open the product filter (**All Products**) and choose a product.
7. Open the status filter (**Any status**) and choose **Open**, **Won** or **Lost**.

## Result
Only matching deals stay on the board. Column counts and totals update to match.

## Tips
- Click **Clear** to remove all filters at once.
- Filters are kept in the page address, so a bookmarked link reopens the same filtered board.
- Save a combination you use often as a saved view.
- The product filter appears only if your role can see products.

## Troubleshooting
- **The board looks empty.** A filter is probably still on. Click **Clear**.
