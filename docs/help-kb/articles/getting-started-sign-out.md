## Overview
Sign out when you finish working, especially on a shared computer.

## Steps
1. Click your avatar at the right end of the top bar to open the **Account menu**.
2. Click **Sign out** at the bottom of the menu.

## Result
Your session ends and the **Sign in** page opens.

## Tips
- If you signed in with a Workeasy360 account, the menu shows two options instead:
  - **Sign out of WorkEasyCRM** ends only your CRM session.
  - **Sign out of WorkEasyCRM and Workeasy360** also takes you to Workeasy360, which asks before signing you out there.
- To sign out other devices, use **Active sessions** on the **Profile & security** page.

## Troubleshooting
- **"Sign out failed"** Check your internet connection and try again.
