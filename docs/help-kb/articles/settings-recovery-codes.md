## Overview
Recovery codes are single-use backup codes. If you lose your phone or authenticator app, you can sign in with one of them instead of the 6-digit code.

## Before you start
- Two-factor authentication must already be turned on for your account.

## Steps
1. Click **Settings** in the left sidebar.
2. In the **Security & Data** card, click **Password & 2FA**.
3. In the **Two-factor authentication** card, find **Recovery codes**.
4. Click **Show codes**.
5. If a **Confirm it's you** window appears, enter your password and click **Confirm**.
6. Click **Copy** and store the codes somewhere safe, such as a password manager.

## Result
Your unused codes are listed. The card shows how many codes are still unused.

## Tips
- To get a fresh set, click **Regenerate**, then **Regenerate** to confirm. All old codes stop working.
- Each code works only once. Regenerate when you are running low.
- At sign-in, type a recovery code in the **Authentication code** box.

## Troubleshooting
- **"All codes have been used."** Click **Regenerate** to create a new set.
