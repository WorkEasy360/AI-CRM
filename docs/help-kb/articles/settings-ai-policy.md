## Overview
The **AI policy** card controls what the assistant may do in your workspace. You can switch generative AI off completely and set a monthly spending budget.

## Before you start
- You need the Owner or Admin role (`ai.settings.manage`).

## Steps
1. Click **Settings** in the left sidebar.
2. In the **AI Assistant** card, click **AI Settings**.
3. In the **AI policy** card, turn the **Generative AI** switch on or off.
4. To set a budget, type an amount in US dollars in **Monthly budget**.
5. Click **Save**.

## Result
The switch saves as soon as you change it, and the budget saves when you click **Save**. The text under **Monthly budget** shows the estimated spend this month and your limit.

## Tips
- When generative AI is off, no customer text leaves your workspace. Ask WorkEasy keeps working: it answers from your CRM records and past conversations, without written analysis or drafts.
- When the budget is reached, Ask WorkEasy switches to CRM answers for the rest of the month instead of failing.
- Set the budget to 0 for no limit.

## Troubleshooting
- **"The budget for this month has been reached."** Raise the **Monthly budget** or wait for the next month.
- **"No access"?** Only Owners and Admins can manage AI settings.
