## Overview
The **Plan & usage** page shows your current plan, how much you use against each limit, the available plans (Free, Express, Premier and 360) and add-ons that raise a single limit.

## Before you start
- The Owner and Admins can open this page. Only the Owner can change the plan or buy add-ons.

## Steps
1. Click **Settings** in the left sidebar.
2. Below the cards, next to **Also in Settings:**, click **Billing & Subscription**.
3. Check **Current plan** and the **Usage** meters.
4. Under **Plans**, choose **Monthly** or **Annual (save up to 22%)**.
5. On the plan you want, enter the number of **Licences** (one per user).
6. Click **Choose** followed by the plan name, for example **Choose Premier**.

## Result
A message says **You're on** followed by the plan name, and the new limits apply straight away.

## Tips
- To raise one limit without changing plan, click **Add** next to an item in the **Add-ons** card (paid plans only). **Remove one** cancels an add-on.
- A meter that is nearly full names the plan that raises the limit.
- **Legacy · full access** means your workspace has the full product without plan limits.

## Troubleshooting
- **The Choose buttons are disabled.** You are not the Owner, or the page says "Plan changes for this workspace are handled by our billing team." In that case, contact support to change plan.
- **"Buy at least that many licences or remove users first."** Enter at least as many licences as you have users.
- **"The Free plan is for one user."** Remove other users and pending invitations first.
