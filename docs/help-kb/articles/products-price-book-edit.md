## Overview
You can rename a price book, switch it off for a while, or archive it when it is no longer needed.

## Before you start
- You need `pricebooks.manage` (Owner, Admin or Sales Manager).

## Steps
1. Click **Settings** in the left sidebar.
2. In the **Also in Settings** links, click **Price books**.
3. Click the **⋯** button on the price book's row (labelled "Actions for" followed by its name).
4. To rename, click **Edit**, change **Name** or **Description**, and click **Save changes**.
5. To switch it off or on, click **Deactivate** or **Activate**.
6. To remove it, click **Archive**, then **Archive** in the confirmation dialog.

## Result
The table shows the new name or the **Active** or **Inactive** status. An archived price book leaves the list.

## Tips
- Inactive and archived price books can no longer be picked on deals.
- Deals that already use an archived price book keep their prices.
- The currency of a price book cannot be edited.
