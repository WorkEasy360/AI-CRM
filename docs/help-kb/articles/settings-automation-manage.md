## Overview
Each automation rule can be switched off without losing it, changed, or deleted. The list also shows how often each rule has run.

## Before you start
- You need the Owner or Admin role (`automation.manage`). Sales Managers see the list but cannot change it.

## Steps
1. Click **Settings** in the left sidebar.
2. Under **Also in Settings:**, click **Automation**.
3. To pause a rule, turn off the switch on its row (labelled **Turn off** and the rule name). Turn it on again to resume.
4. To change a rule, click the **⋯** button on its row (labelled **Actions for** and the rule name), then click **Edit**.
5. Change the settings and click **Save**.
6. To remove a rule, click the **⋯** button, then **Delete**, then **Delete** to confirm.

## Result
You see "Automation updated" or "Automation deleted". Each row shows "Ran N times · last" date, or "Has not run yet", and how many steps are waiting.

## Tips
- Deleting a rule drops its waiting steps. Tasks, notifications and messages it already created stay.
- "Last run skipped" under a rule explains why the latest run did nothing.

## Troubleshooting
- **"Could not change the rule"?** Try again; your role may not allow changes.
- **Switch is greyed out?** Your role can only view automations.
