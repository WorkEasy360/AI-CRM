## Overview
Add articles about how your team works, or change a WorkEasy CRM guide article for your workspace only. WorkEasy AI and the Help Center use them straight away once published.

## Before you start
- You need the **Owner** or **Admin** role.

## Steps
1. Click **Settings** in the left sidebar.
2. In the **AI Assistant** card, click **Knowledge Base**.
3. Click **New article**, or click **Customise** next to a guide article.
4. Fill in **Title**, **Question it answers**, **Module** and **Page (route)**.
5. Write the article in **Markdown**, with the exact button names in bold.
6. Click **Preview** to check it.
7. Set **Status** to **Published (WorkEasy AI answers from it)**.
8. Click **Save and publish**.

## Result
The article appears under **Your workspace's articles** and in the Help Center. A customised article replaces the guide article for your workspace only.

## Tips
- Every save keeps a version. Open the article, click **Version history** and click **Restore** to go back.
- Deleting a customised article brings back the original guide article.
