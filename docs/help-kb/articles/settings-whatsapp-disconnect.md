## Overview
Disconnecting WhatsApp stops everyone from sending or receiving WhatsApp messages in the CRM. Do this to switch to a different number or to replace the access token.

## Before you start
- You need the Owner or Admin role (`whatsapp.manage`).

## Steps
1. Click **Settings** in the left sidebar.
2. In the **Communication** card, click **WhatsApp Integration**.
3. At the bottom of the **Business account** card, click **Disconnect**.
4. In **Disconnect WhatsApp?**, click **Disconnect**.
5. If **Confirm it's you** appears, enter your **Password** and click **Confirm** (or click **Confirm with Workeasy360**).

## Result
You see "WhatsApp disconnected" and the card shows **Not connected** with the connection form again. Conversation history stays on the records.

## Tips
- To use a new number or token, disconnect first, then fill in **Phone number ID** and **Access token** again and click **Connect WhatsApp**.
- Recorded message templates are kept; you do not need to add them again.

## Troubleshooting
- **No Disconnect button?** Only Owners and Admins can disconnect WhatsApp.
