## Overview
The bottom row of the Dashboard shows your day: today's scheduled follow-ups, your open tasks and what happened recently.

## Before you start
- Your role needs access to Activities. Without it, these panels say **Your role does not include access to this data.**

## Steps
1. Click **Dashboard** in the left sidebar.
2. Look at **Today's Follow-ups**. It lists up to five of today's timed tasks, calls and meetings; **View All** shows the total in brackets.
3. Use a row's buttons: the phone calls the contact, the message icon opens WhatsApp, and the tick marks it done.
4. In **Pending Tasks**, tick a task to complete it. The label shows **Overdue**, **Today**, **Tomorrow** or the date.
5. Click any item to open it on the Activities page.
6. For counts of overdue and upcoming work, click **More insights** and read **Upcoming & Overdue**.

## Result
You know what is due now and what has slipped, and can act on each item in one click.

## Tips
- Only activities with a start time appear in **Today's Follow-ups**, and they are always your own.
- **Pending Tasks** follows **My Records** or **All Records**.

## Troubleshooting
- **"Nothing scheduled for today."** You have no timed activities today. Use **Schedule Follow-up** in **Quick Actions** to plan one.
- **"Your follow-ups are unavailable right now."** Reload the page.
