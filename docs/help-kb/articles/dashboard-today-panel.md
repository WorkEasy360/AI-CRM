## Overview
The right column of the Dashboard shows your day: today's scheduled activities and a count of overdue and upcoming work.

## Before you start
- Your role needs access to Activities. Without it, these panels say **Your role does not include access to this data.**

## Steps
1. Click **Dashboard** in the left sidebar.
2. Look at **Today's Agenda** on the right. It lists up to five of today's timed tasks, calls and meetings, with the start time; the heading shows the total in brackets.
3. Click an item to open it on the Activities page.
4. Click **View all** to see your full day in Activities.
5. Below it, read **Upcoming & Overdue**: **Overdue Activities**, **Activities Due This Week** and **Deals Closing This Week**.
6. Click a line to open the matching filtered list.

## Result
You know what is due now and what has slipped, and can open each item in one click.

## Tips
- Only activities with a start time appear in **Today's Agenda**.

## Troubleshooting
- **"Nothing scheduled for today."** You have no timed activities today. Use **Add Task** or **Schedule Meeting** in **Quick Actions** to plan one.
- **"Your agenda is unavailable right now."** Reload the page.
