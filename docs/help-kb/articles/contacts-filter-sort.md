## Overview
Narrow the people list to what you need, such as your own contacts, one status, or contacts without a company, and choose the order.

## Steps
1. Click **Contacts** in the left sidebar, then **People (Contacts)**.
2. Click the **Individuals** tab.
3. In the view menu at the left of the toolbar, choose **All contacts**, **My contacts** or **Archived contacts**.
4. Click **Filters**.
5. In the status menu, pick a status (Lead, Prospect, Qualified, Customer or Inactive).
6. In the company menu, pick **Any company**, **Has a company** or **No company**.
7. In the sort menu at the right, choose an order such as **Newest first**, **Recently updated** or **Recent activity**.

## Result
The list updates immediately. The **Filters** button shows how many extra filters are on.

## Tips
- Click **Clear** to remove all filters and the search text.
- You can also click a column header such as **Name** or **Status** to sort.
- On **Companies**, **Filters** offers status, **Industry** and company size.
- Save a filter set you use often as a saved view.
