## Overview
Mark a task, call or meeting as complete when it is done. Completed items move out of your open lists, and you can reopen them later.

## Before you start
- You need `activities.update` for the activity. A Sales Representative can complete their own activities; Owner, Admin and Sales Manager can complete anyone's.

## Steps
1. Click **Activities** in the left sidebar.
2. Find the activity in the table (use **Search activities…** if needed).
3. Click the **⋮** actions button at the end of its row.
4. Click **Mark complete**.

## Result
The status changes to **Completed** and the title is shown struck through. To undo, open the same menu and click **Reopen**.

## Tips
- In the grouped list and in **My Day**, tick the checkbox at the left of a row to mark it as done.
- When you tick a call that has no outcome yet, a **How did the call go?** box opens. Pick an **Outcome**, add a **Note (optional)** and click **Mark as done**.
- A repeating task creates its next occurrence when you complete it.
- To complete many at once, see the bulk actions article.

## Troubleshooting
- **No Mark complete option?** You can only view this activity. Ask its owner or a manager to complete it.
