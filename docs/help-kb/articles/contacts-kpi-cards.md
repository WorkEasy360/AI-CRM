## Overview
Five summary cards at the top of the Contacts and Companies pages count your records: **Total Contacts** (people plus companies), **Individuals** (people), **Companies**, **Favorites** (records starred by you) and **Tagged** (records with at least one tag).

## Steps
1. Click **Contacts** in the left sidebar, then **People (Contacts)**.
2. Read the cards below the page title.
3. Click the **Individuals**, **Companies** or **Favorites** card to open that list.

## Result
You see the matching list. The percentage on Total Contacts, Individuals and Companies compares records created in the last period (for example the last 30 days) with the period before it.

## Tips
- Counts include only records your role lets you see, so two users may see different numbers.
- Favorites are personal: each user has their own.
- The Total Contacts and Tagged cards are not clickable. Use the **Tags** tab to list records with a given tag.

## Troubleshooting
- **Companies card shows "No access"?** Your role cannot view companies.
