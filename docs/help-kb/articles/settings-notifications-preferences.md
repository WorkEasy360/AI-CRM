## Overview
Choose which events you are told about, and where: in the app (the bell menu) or by email to your sign-in address. These settings are personal; they do not change anyone else's alerts.

## Steps
1. Click **Settings** in the left sidebar.
2. In the **Communication** card, click **Notifications**.
3. In the **Alerts** card, find the event in the **Event** column.
4. Turn the **In-app** switch on or off for that event.
5. Turn the **Email** switch on or off for that event.

## Result
Each change saves straight away and a "Preferences saved" message appears.

## Tips
- Events you can control: Task due, Meeting approaching, Call scheduled, Deal assigned to me, Deal inactivity warning, Customer replied, Deal at high risk, Integration needs attention and Automation.
- To change how long a deal can sit without activity before you are warned, use the **Deal inactivity** card on the same page.

## Troubleshooting
- **"Could not save preferences"?** The switch returns to its saved position. Try again or reload the page.
