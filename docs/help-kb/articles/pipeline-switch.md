## Overview
When your workspace has more than one pipeline, the **Pipeline** page shows one pipeline at a time. It opens on the default pipeline, and you can switch at any time.

## Steps
1. Click **Pipeline** in the left sidebar.
2. Make sure **Kanban** view is selected.
3. Click **Filters**, then open the **Pipeline** drop-down (next to **Search deals…**).
4. Choose the pipeline you want.

## Result
The board, the summary (**Total Deals**, **Open Pipeline Value** and so on) and the **List** view now show that pipeline's deals.

## Tips
- On wide screens a **Pipelines** column on the left lists every pipeline. Click a name to switch. A lock icon marks restricted pipelines.
- If the pipeline has sub-pipelines, small buttons above the board switch between the parent and its sub-pipelines.
- The selected pipeline is kept in the page address, so you can bookmark or share the link.

## Troubleshooting
- **A pipeline is missing from the list.** It may be restricted to other teams or roles, or archived. Ask a Sales Manager or Admin.
