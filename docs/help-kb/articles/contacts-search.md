## Overview
Use the search box on the Contacts page to find people and companies by name, company, phone or email.

## Steps
1. Click **Contacts** in the left sidebar, then **People (Contacts)**.
2. On the **All Contacts** tab, click the box **Search contacts by name, company, phone, email…**.
3. Type part of a name, company, phone number or email.
4. Click the name in the results to open the record.

## Result
The list filters as you type and shows matching people and companies.

## Tips
- Use the **Type** menu next to the search box to show only **Individuals** or **Companies**.
- The **Individuals** tab has its own **Search contacts…** box that works together with filters and sorting; the Companies page has **Search companies…**.
- Archived records are left out. On the Individuals tab, switch the view menu to **Archived contacts** to search them.

## Troubleshooting
- **"No contacts match"?** Check the spelling, type fewer characters, or clear filters.
