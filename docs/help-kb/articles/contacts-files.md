## Overview
Keep proposals, contracts, ID copies and other documents with the person they belong to.

## Before you start
- You need upload permission and must be able to edit the contact. Sales Representatives can upload to contacts they own.
- Files can be PDF, image, text or Office documents, up to 10 MB each.

## Steps
1. Click **Contacts** in the left sidebar, then **People (Contacts)**.
2. Click the person's name to open the contact.
3. Click the **Files** tab.
4. Click **Choose a file**, or drag a file onto the box that says "Drop a file here, or".
5. Select the file on your computer.

## Result
A "File uploaded" message appears and the file is listed with its size. Use the download button to open it.

## Tips
- Delete a file with the bin icon next to it. Sales Representatives can delete only files they uploaded.
- The Files tab is on contact pages; company pages do not have one.

## Troubleshooting
- **"File too large"?** Files are limited to 10 MB each. Compress or split the file.
- **No upload box?** The contact is archived, or you cannot edit it.
