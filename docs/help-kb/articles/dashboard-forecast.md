## Overview
The Forecast shows open deals by expected close date, weighted by their probability. It is arithmetic on today's pipeline, not a prediction.

## Before you start
- Your role needs the Reports permission. Owner, Admin, Sales Manager and Viewer see all deals; a Sales Representative sees team deals.

## Steps
1. Click **Dashboard** in the left sidebar.
2. Click the **Forecast** tab next to **Overview**.
3. In **Period**, choose **This month**, **This quarter** or **Custom**. For **Custom**, pick the **From** and **To** dates.
4. If you have several pipelines, choose one in **Pipeline**.
5. In **Group by**, choose **Stage**, **Salesperson**, **Team** or **Pipeline**.
6. Read the tiles: **Pipeline value**, **Weighted**, **Committed** (deals at 75% probability or more), **Won so far** and **Expected revenue** (weighted plus won).
7. Review the **By month** chart and the breakdown table below it. Click a row to open those deals.

## Result
You see how much revenue is likely in the period and where it comes from.

## Tips
- Click **Review close dates** to fix open deals that have no close date or are overdue; they make the forecast less accurate.
- The same view is the **Forecast** tab on the Pipeline page.

## Troubleshooting
- **"Forecast is not available for your role".** Ask an administrator for the Reports permission.
- **"Choose a date range".** Pick both dates for a custom period.
