## Overview
Remove a person when they no longer work with you. They lose access immediately, but the records they own and their history stay in the CRM.

## Before you start
- Only the Owner and Admins can remove users. Only the Owner can remove another Owner.
- You cannot remove yourself, and the organization must keep at least one Owner.

## Steps
1. Click **Settings** in the left sidebar.
2. In the **Workspace** card, click **Users & Teams**.
3. Click the **…** actions button at the end of the person's row.
4. Click **Remove user**.
5. In **Remove user?**, click **Remove user**.
6. If a **Confirm it's you** window appears, enter your password and click **Confirm**.

## Result
A message says **User removed** and the status changes to **Disabled**. The person is signed out everywhere, removed from all teams, and any personal mailbox they connected is disconnected.

## Tips
- Records they owned stay in the CRM and still show them as owner.
- If you may need them back soon, use **Suspend** instead; removal cannot be undone from the menu.
- To bring a removed person back, invite them again.

## Troubleshooting
- **"The organization must keep at least one owner."** Give another member the Owner role first.
- **The row has no actions button.** Removed users have no further actions.
