## Overview
WorkEasy runs in your web browser on computers, tablets and phones. On small screens the layout adapts: the sidebar opens from a menu button, and settings pages use a drop-down instead of a side menu.

## Steps
1. On your phone, open your WorkEasy address in an up-to-date browser and sign in.

## Result
You see the same workspace, data and permissions as on your computer.

## Tips
- On a phone, a bar at the bottom of the screen has **Pipeline**, **Contacts**, **Activities**, **Search** and **New**. Tap **Open navigation** (the menu button at the top left) for the full sidebar.
- Your browser can install WorkEasy as an app: use its "Install app" or "Add to Home screen" option. It then opens full screen from your home screen, starting on the pipeline.
- Use a current version of your browser with cookies and JavaScript turned on.

## Troubleshooting
- **"You're offline".** WorkEasy needs an internet connection to load your pipeline and records. Nothing is stored on the device. Reconnect and reload the page.
- **I can't find WorkEasy in the app store.** Use the browser install option above instead.
