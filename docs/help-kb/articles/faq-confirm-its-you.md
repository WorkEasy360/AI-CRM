## Overview
Some changes are sensitive, such as changing a role, inviting an Admin, removing or suspending a user, changing organization details or turning on two-factor authentication. Before WorkEasy saves them, it checks that the person at the keyboard is really you.

## Steps
1. In the **Confirm it's you** window, enter your **Password** and click **Confirm**.

## Result
Your change is saved. You will not be asked again for about 10 minutes.

## Tips
- If you click **Cancel**, nothing is changed.
- If you sign in with a Workeasy360 account, the window shows **Confirm with Workeasy360** instead. You sign in at Workeasy360 again and come back to the same page; then repeat the action.

## Troubleshooting
- **"Enter your password to continue."** The password box was empty.
- **"Could not confirm your password."** Check the password and try again. After several wrong tries, wait a minute before trying again.
