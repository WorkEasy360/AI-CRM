## Overview
Global search finds contacts, companies, deals, activities, tickets and products from any page, so you do not have to open each list.

## Before you start
- Your role needs the search permission. Owner, Admin, Sales Manager, Sales Representative and Viewer all have it by default.
- Results only include records your role is allowed to see.

## Steps
1. Click the search box in the top bar (**Search anything… (contacts, companies, deals, activities, tickets)**), or press **Ctrl K** (**⌘ K** on a Mac).
2. Type at least 2 characters, for example part of a name, company or email.
3. Wait a moment for results. They are grouped under headings such as **Contacts**, **Companies**, **Deals**, **Activities**, **Tickets** and **Products**.
4. Use the **↑** and **↓** keys to move, or point with the mouse.
5. Press **Enter** or click a result to open it.

## Result
The record opens. Activities open on the Activities page and tickets open in the Helpdesk.

## Tips
- Press **Esc** to close search.
- You can also open search from the **Help** menu (**?**) → **Search everything**.
- On a phone, tap **Search** in the bottom bar.

## Troubleshooting
- **"No results for …"** Check the spelling or try fewer words. The record may also belong to someone outside your visibility.
- **"Too many searches in a short time."** Wait a few seconds and try again.
- **"You don't have permission to search."** Ask an administrator to add the search permission to your role.
