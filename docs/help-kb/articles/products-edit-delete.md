## Overview
You can change a product's name, price, tax or stock at any time. A product you no longer sell can be archived (deleted) and restored later.

## Before you start
- Editing needs `products.update`; archiving and restoring need `products.delete`. Owner and Admin have both.

## Steps
1. Click **Products** in the left sidebar.
2. Find the product, using **Search products…** if needed.
3. Click the **⋯** button at the end of its row (labelled "Actions for" followed by the product name).
4. To edit, click **Edit**, change the fields in **Edit product** and click **Save changes**.
5. To delete, click **Archive**.

## Result
An edit shows "Product updated". An archived product leaves the active list and shows an **Archived** badge.

## Tips
- To restore, choose **Archived products** in the first drop-down, open the row's **⋯** menu and click **Restore**.
- You can do the same from the product page: click **Edit**, or the **More actions** button for **Archive** or **Restore**.
- Setting **Status** to **Inactive** keeps the product but hides it from the deal product picker.
- Changing a product's price does not change deals that already include it.

## Troubleshooting
- **"This record was changed by someone else".** Click **Reload** in the form, then make your change again.
