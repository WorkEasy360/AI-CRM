## Overview
The List view shows the deals of the selected pipeline as a sortable table. It is useful for scanning many deals, sorting by amount or close date, and finding archived deals.

## Steps
1. Click **Pipeline** in the left sidebar.
2. Click **List** in the view tabs.
3. Use the first drop-down to choose **All deals**, **My deals** or **Archived deals**.
4. Type in **Search deals…** to find a deal by name.
5. Pick an order in the **Sort** drop-down, for example **Closing soonest** or **Amount high–low**. You can also click a sortable column header.
6. Click a row to open the deal.

## Result
The table lists **Deal name**, **Stage**, **Company**, **Amount**, **Probability**, **Weighted**, **Expected close**, **Next activity**, **Owner** and **Status** (some columns appear only on wider screens). Clicking a row opens the full deal page.

## Tips
- Click **Filters** to filter by status (**Open**, **Won**, **Lost**) or by stage.
- When there are more deals than fit on one page, click **Load more** at the bottom of the table.
- The List view shows the pipeline selected on the **Kanban** view. To change pipeline, switch it there first.

## Troubleshooting
- **"No deals match these filters".** Click **Clear filters**.
