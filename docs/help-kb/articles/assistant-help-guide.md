## Overview
Ask WorkEasy AI how to do anything in WorkEasy CRM, such as "How do I create a pipeline?" or "contact ko company se kaise link kare". It answers from the WorkEasy CRM help guide with the exact steps, and takes you to the right screen.

## Steps
1. Click **Ask AI Now** on the **WorkEasy AI** card at the bottom of the left sidebar.
2. Click one of the **Help with this page** questions, or type your own question in English, Hindi or Hinglish.
3. Press **Enter** or click **Ask**.
4. Follow the numbered steps in the answer. The grey chips show the menu path.
5. Click **Take me there** to open that screen. The button to start with is highlighted for a few seconds.
6. Click a topic under **Related topics** to ask about the next step.
7. Under **Was this helpful?**, click **Helpful** or **Not helpful**, and add a comment if you like.

## Result
You see the steps for your task and can jump straight to the screen where you do it.

## Tips
- Ask "How do I add one here?" and WorkEasy AI uses the page you are on.
- If your role cannot use a feature, the answer says so; ask your workspace admin for access.

## Troubleshooting
- **"I couldn't find this in the WorkEasy CRM help guide yet."** The guide has no article for it. Your question is passed to your workspace admins, who can add one.
