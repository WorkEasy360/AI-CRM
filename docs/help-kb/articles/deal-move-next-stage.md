## Overview
The **Deal Details** panel has a **Move to Next Stage** button that moves an open deal one stage forward in one click.

## Before you start
- You need `deals.change_stage` for the deal. A Sales Representative can move only their own deals.

## Steps
1. Click **Pipeline** in the left sidebar.
2. On the **Kanban** view, click the deal's name on its card.
3. In the **Deal Details** panel, click **Move to Next Stage** at the bottom.

## Result
The deal moves to the next stage to the right, and its stage label and probability update.

## Tips
- Lost stages are skipped. From the last open stage, the next stage is the Won stage, so the button marks the deal as won.
- To jump to any stage, use the **Stage** drop-down at the top of the panel.
- The button is shown only for open deals.
