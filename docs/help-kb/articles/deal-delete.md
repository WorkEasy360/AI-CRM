## Overview
Deleting a deal removes it from the board and every list. Nothing is erased: the deal is kept as an archived record. If the deal was simply lost, mark it as lost instead so it counts in your win/loss figures.

## Before you start
- You need `deals.delete`. A Sales Representative can delete only their own deals; Sales Manager, Admin and Owner can delete any deal.

## Steps
1. Click **Pipeline** in the left sidebar.
2. Click the deal's name on its card, then click **Open full deal**.
3. Click **Delete** at the top right of the deal page.
4. Click **Delete** in the confirmation dialog.

## Result
The deal disappears from the board and lists, and its page shows a **Deleted** badge.

## Tips
- You can also delete from the **Edit Deal** panel: click **Delete** at the bottom left, then **Delete deal**.
- To find deleted deals, open **List View** and choose **Archived deals** in the first drop-down.

## Troubleshooting
- **No Delete button.** You can delete only deals your role allows, and a deal that is already deleted shows no Delete button.
