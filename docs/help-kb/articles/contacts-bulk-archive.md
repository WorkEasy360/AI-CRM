## Overview
Select several contacts or companies in the list and archive them, or restore archived ones, in one action.

## Before you start
- Bulk actions need the bulk update permission. Owner, Admin and Sales Manager have it; Sales Representative and Viewer do not.
- Bulk actions work in the **List** view only.

## Steps
1. Click **Contacts** in the left sidebar, then **People (Contacts)**.
2. Click the **Individuals** tab.
3. Tick the checkbox at the start of each row you want, or tick the header checkbox to select all loaded rows.
4. In the bar that appears above the list, click **Archive**.
5. In the confirmation box, click **Archive**.

## Result
A message such as "5 of 5 updated" appears and the archived contacts leave the list.

## Tips
- To restore in bulk, first switch the view menu to **Archived contacts**, select the rows, then click **Restore**.
- The same bar is available on the **Companies** list.
- Click the **X** at the right of the bar to clear the selection.

## Troubleshooting
- **"Some records were skipped because you cannot change them."** Those records are outside what your role may change; they were left as they were.
- **No checkboxes?** Your role does not have bulk update permission.
