## Overview
Star the people and companies you work with most so you can reach them from the **Favorites** tab. Favorites are personal; other users do not see your stars.

## Steps
1. Click **Contacts** in the left sidebar, then **People (Contacts)**.
2. Stay on the **All Contacts** tab.
3. Click the ⋮ button at the end of the person's or company's row.
4. Click **Add to favorites**.

## Result
A star appears next to the name, the **Favorites** count goes up, and the record is listed on the **Favorites** tab.

## Tips
- To unstar, open the same menu and click **Remove from favorites**.
- On the **Individuals** tab the row menu also has **Add to favorites**.
- Starred companies appear in Favorites too; star them from the All Contacts list.

## Troubleshooting
- **"Could not update favourites"?** The record may have been archived or you may no longer have access to it. Refresh the page and try again.
