## Overview
WorkEasy can warn you when a deal you own has had no activity for a number of days. You choose the number of days, or turn the warning off.

## Steps
1. Click **Settings** in the left sidebar.
2. In the **Communication** card, click **Notifications**.
3. In the **Deal inactivity** card, type a number of days in **Warn after (days)**, between 0 and 365.
4. Click **Save**.

## Result
A "Preferences saved" message appears and the hint under the field reads, for example, "You will be warned after 14 days without activity." With 0 it reads "Inactivity warnings are off."

## Tips
- Set the value to 0 to turn the warning off.
- The warning applies only to deals you own.
- Choose whether the warning arrives in the app or by email with the **Deal inactivity warning** row in the **Alerts** card.

## Troubleshooting
- **"Enter a whole number between 0 and 365."** Remove decimals or reduce the number.
- **Save is greyed out?** The value has not changed.
