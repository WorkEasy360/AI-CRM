## Overview
The activity table lists tasks, calls and meetings with their related record, owner, due date and status. Filters above it narrow the list.

## Before you start
- You need `activities.view` (all built-in roles). A Sales Representative sees their own and their team's activities.

## Steps
1. Click **Activities** in the left sidebar.
2. Click a tab: **All Activities**, **Tasks**, **Calls**, **Meetings** or **Follow-ups**.
3. Type in **Search activities…** to search titles and descriptions.
4. On **All Activities** or **Follow-ups**, pick a type in the **All Types** list.
5. Pick a status in **All Status**: **Open**, **In Progress**, **Completed** or **Cancelled**.
6. Pick an owner in **All Owners**, or choose **Me**.
7. Pick a date range: **Today**, **This Week**, **This Month** or **Custom**. For **Custom**, set the **From** and **To** dates.

## Result
The table shows only matching rows, with columns **Activity**, **Related To**, **Owner**, **Due Date & Time** and **Status**. Click **Load more** at the bottom to see more rows.

## Tips
- Click a day in the small month calendar on the right to show only that day's activities.
- Filters are kept in the page address, so you can bookmark or share a filtered view.
- The **Grouped list** button shows the same activities grouped by **Overdue**, **Today**, **Tomorrow**, **Later**, **No date** and **Completed**, with a **Due** filter and a **Sort** list.

## Troubleshooting
- **"No activities match these filters"** Reset the lists to **All Types**, **All Status**, **All Owners** and **Any time**, and clear the search box.
