## Overview
A ticket records a customer's support request so your team can track it until it is resolved.

## Before you start
- You need `tickets.create`. Owner, Admin, Sales Manager and Sales Representative have it; Viewer does not.

## Steps
1. Click **Helpdesk** in the left sidebar.
2. Click **Create Ticket**.
3. Enter a **Subject**.
4. Choose a **Category**: **Technical**, **Billing**, **Account**, **Support**, **Sales** or **Other** (default **Support**).
5. Choose a **Priority**: **Low**, **Medium**, **High** or **Urgent** (default **Medium**).
6. In **Requester (contact)**, search for and pick the customer. If they are not a contact, type their name in **Or requester name**.
7. Optional: describe the problem in **Description**.
8. Click **Create Ticket**.

## Result
A "Ticket created" message appears with the ticket number (for example #TK-1044). The **Ticket Details** panel opens, and the ticket starts as **Open**, assigned to you.

## Tips
- You can also click **Create** in the top bar and choose **Ticket**.
- Link a contact if you want to reply by email or WhatsApp from the ticket.
- The **Description** becomes the first customer message in the **Conversation**.
- Priority sets the SLA deadline, so pick it carefully.

## Troubleshooting
- **"Subject is required."** Enter a subject before saving.
- **No Create Ticket button?** Your role cannot create tickets.
