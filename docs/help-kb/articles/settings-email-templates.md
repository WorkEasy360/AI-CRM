## Overview
Email templates save a subject and body you send often. Placeholders such as `{{first_name}}` are filled in for the contact and deal when you pick the template in the email composer.

## Before you start
- You need the Owner, Admin or Sales Manager role (`email.templates_manage`) to create, edit or delete templates. Other roles can use shared templates.

## Steps
1. Click **Settings** in the left sidebar.
2. In the **Communication** card, click **Email Settings**.
3. In the **Email templates** card, click **New template**.
4. Type a **Name**, for example "Follow-up after demo".
5. Type a **Subject**.
6. Write the **Body** (plain text).
7. Under **Placeholders (click to insert)**, click a placeholder to add it to the body: `{{first_name}}`, `{{last_name}}`, `{{full_name}}`, `{{company}}`, `{{deal_name}}` or `{{owner_name}}`.
8. Turn on **Share with the whole team** if everyone should see it.
9. Click **Create template**.

## Result
A "Template created" message appears and the template is listed with its **Subject**, **Visibility** (**Shared** or **Private**) and **Updated** date.

## Tips
- To change or remove a template, click its **⋯** button, then **Edit** (and **Save changes**) or **Delete** (and **Delete template**).
- Private templates are visible only to you.
- The same section also appears on **Message Templates**.

## Troubleshooting
- **"Write the message body."** The body cannot be empty.
- **No New template button?** Your role cannot manage email templates.
