## Overview
On the AI Assistant page you can speak your question instead of typing it. Your browser turns speech into text in the question box.

## Before you start
- Use a browser with speech recognition, such as Chrome, Edge or Safari. In other browsers the microphone button does not appear.
- Allow microphone access when the browser asks.

## Steps
1. Click **AI Assistant** in the left sidebar.
2. Click the microphone button (**Speak your question**) next to the question box.
3. Say your question clearly.
4. Stop speaking; recording ends on its own. To stop early, click the button again (**Stop voice input**).
5. Check the text in the question box and correct it if needed.
6. Click **Ask** (the arrow button).

## Result
Your spoken question is sent like a typed one and the answer appears in the conversation.

## Tips
- The browser listens in your browser's language setting.
- Voice is only on the AI Assistant page, not in the side panel.

## Troubleshooting
- **No microphone button.** Your browser does not support speech recognition. Type your question instead.
- **Nothing is typed.** Check that the browser has permission to use your microphone.
