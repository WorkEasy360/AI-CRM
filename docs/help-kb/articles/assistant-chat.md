## Overview
The WorkEasy AI Assistant answers questions about your customers, deals and activities using the CRM records you are allowed to see. It can also draft messages and suggest next steps.

## Before you start
- Your role needs AI Assistant access. Owner, Admin, Sales Manager, Sales Representative and Viewer all have it by default.

## Steps
1. Click **AI Assistant** in the left sidebar.
2. Click the box at the bottom that says **Ask anything… (e.g., create follow-up, write message, show report)**.
3. Type your question, for example "Which deals are at risk?" or "What did Acme say about pricing?".
4. Press **Enter** or click **Ask** (the arrow button).
5. Wait while it shows **Looking through your CRM…**.
6. Read the answer. To continue, type a follow-up question or click one of the suggestion buttons under the answer.

## Result
The answer appears as a chat bubble below your question. Follow-up questions keep the context of the conversation.

## Tips
- Good questions: "What should I follow up today?", "Summarize my pipeline.", "Show high-value deals closing this month.", "Catch me up on Acme".
- You can also click a suggestion under the chat, such as **Write a follow-up message**, **Show deal forecast** or **Team performance**.
- The assistant does not create or change records itself. Use **Create a task** or the create forms for that.

## Troubleshooting
- **"AI Assistant is not part of your role".** Ask a workspace administrator to give your role access.
- **An error appears instead of an answer.** You may have asked too many questions in a minute. Wait a moment and ask again.
