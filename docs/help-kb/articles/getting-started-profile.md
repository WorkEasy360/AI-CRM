## Overview
The **Account menu** (your avatar at the top right) shows who you are signed in as and links to your account's security page.

## Steps
1. Click your avatar at the right end of the top bar to open the **Account menu**.
2. Read the top of the menu: your name, your email, and your current workspace with your role (for example, "Acme · Sales Manager").
3. Click **Profile & security**.
4. On the **Security** page, use the **Password**, **Two-factor authentication** and **Active sessions** cards to manage your sign-in.

## Result
The **Security** page opens with your password, two-factor authentication and the devices currently signed in to your account.

## Tips
- The same menu has **Settings**, **Theme**, your workspaces (if you belong to more than one) and **Sign out**.
- If you sign in with a Workeasy360 account, the Security page tells you to change your password and two-step verification in Workeasy360 instead.

## Troubleshooting
- **I want to change my name or photo.** The app has no screen for editing your name or photo today. Your name comes from your sign-up or invitation.
