## Overview
You can show the CRM in a light or dark colour scheme, or let it follow your device's setting.

## Steps
1. Click your avatar at the right end of the top bar to open the **Account menu**.
2. Find the **Theme** section.
3. Choose **Light**, **Dark** or **System**.

## Result
The app switches colours straight away. **System** follows the light or dark setting of your computer or phone.

## Tips
- The theme is saved in this browser only. It does not change what other members see, and you may need to choose it again on another device or browser.
- Choose **System** if your device switches to dark mode automatically in the evening.
