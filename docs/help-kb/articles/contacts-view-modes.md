## Overview
The Individuals tab can show your people in five ways: **List**, **Kanban**, **Sheet**, **Calendar** and **Chart**.

## Steps
1. Click **Contacts** in the left sidebar, then **People (Contacts)**.
2. Click the **Individuals** tab.
3. Click **List**, **Kanban**, **Sheet**, **Calendar** or **Chart** in the view switch above the toolbar.
4. In **Kanban**, use **Group by** to choose which field makes the columns.
5. In **Calendar**, use **Place by** to choose the date field.
6. In **Chart**, choose a measure and a **Group by** field.

## Result
The contacts appear in the chosen layout. Your current filters still apply.

## Tips
- **Kanban** groups by status by default. Drag a card to another column to change that field.
- **Sheet** works like a spreadsheet: double-click a cell (or press Enter) to edit, and use copy, paste and Ctrl+D to fill down. Edits wait until you click **Save** (for example "Save 3 rows") or **Discard**.
- In **List**, click **Columns** to choose and reorder the columns.
- Save a layout you like as a saved view.
