## Overview
The Message Templates page shows your email templates and your approved WhatsApp templates in one place, for follow-ups and automations.

## Before you start
- Any member can open this page and see the templates. Creating or changing email templates needs the Owner, Admin or Sales Manager role. Adding or removing WhatsApp templates needs the Owner or Admin role.

## Steps
1. Click **Settings** in the left sidebar.
2. In the **Communication** card, click **Message Templates**.
3. In the **Email templates** card, review your email templates, or click **New template** to create one.
4. In the **Message templates** card (WhatsApp), review the approved templates, or click **Add template** to record one.

## Result
You see both lists. Email templates show **Subject**, **Visibility** and **Updated**; WhatsApp templates show **Language**, **Category**, **Placeholders**, **Status** and **Added**.

## Tips
- The same sections also appear on **Email Settings** and **WhatsApp Integration**; changes show in both places.
- WhatsApp templates must be approved in Meta before you add them here.

## Troubleshooting
- **"Templates are not part of your role"?** Ask a workspace administrator for access.
- **No New template or Add template button?** Your role can view templates but not manage them.
