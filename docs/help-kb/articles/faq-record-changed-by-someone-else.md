## Overview
WorkEasy protects you from overwriting a colleague's work. If someone else saved the same contact, company, deal, product or activity after you opened it, your save is stopped and you see "This record was changed by someone else. Reload to see their changes before saving." For an activity, the message starts "This activity was changed by someone else".

## Steps
1. Click **Reload** in the yellow message inside the form.

## Result
The form shows the latest saved values. Make your change again and save.

## Tips
- On the pipeline board, the message is **Someone else changed this deal**. The board refreshes with the latest version; drag the deal again.
- In the table (sheet) view, rows changed by someone else show their values and the cells you edited are marked so you can redo them.
- Keep record forms open only while you are editing to avoid conflicts.

## Troubleshooting
- **It keeps happening.** Another person or an automation rule may be updating the same record. Agree with your colleagues who edits it, or try again a little later.
