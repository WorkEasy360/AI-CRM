## Overview
The Contacts page has tabs that show people and companies together or separately. Each tab shows its record count in brackets.

## Steps
1. Click **Contacts** in the left sidebar, then **People (Contacts)**.
2. Click **All Contacts** to see people and companies in one list, newest updates first.
3. Optionally use the **Type** menu to show **All Types**, **Individuals** or **Companies** only.
4. Click **Individuals** to see only people, with views, filters, sorting and bulk actions.
5. Click **Companies** to open the companies list.
6. Click **Favorites** to see only the people and companies you starred.
7. Click **Tags** and pick a tag to see records with that tag.

## Result
The list changes to the tab you picked. Click a name to open the record.

## Tips
- All Contacts and Favorites show Name, Type, Company Name, Phone, Email, Tags and Last Activity. Use the row ⋮ menu for **View** and **Add to favorites**.
- Edit, archive, filters and saved views are on the **Individuals** and **Companies** tabs.
- The **Tags** tab appears only when your workspace has tags.

## Troubleshooting
- **No Companies tab?** Your role cannot view companies.
