## Overview
Every account starts with its own workspace. If you run more than one business, you can create another organization. Its data is kept completely separate from your other organizations.

## Before you start
- The **Organization Details** link appears in Settings for Owners and Admins. Other members can open the page at **/settings/general** to reach the **Your organizations** card.

## Steps
1. Click **Settings** in the left sidebar.
2. In the **Workspace** card, click **Organization Details**.
3. Scroll to the **Your organizations** card.
4. Click **New organization**.
5. Enter the **Organization name**.
6. Choose a **Base currency**.
7. Choose a **Time zone**.
8. Click **Create organization**.

## Result
You become the Owner of the new organization, it starts with a ready-to-use sales pipeline, and the CRM opens inside it.

## Tips
- To go back to another organization, click **Open** next to it in the **Your organizations** card. Switching changes everything you see in the CRM.
- Badges next to each organization show your role there.

## Troubleshooting
- **The Open button is disabled.** Your membership in that organization is not active (for example, it was suspended). Ask that organization's Owner or Admin.
