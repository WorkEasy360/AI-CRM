## Overview
A pipeline is the ordered set of stages your deals move through. Create a separate pipeline for each sales process, for example new business, renewals or partnerships.

## Before you start
- You need the manage-pipelines permission (`pipelines.manage`). The Owner, Admin and Sales Manager roles have it.
- Your plan sets how many team pipelines you can have.

## Steps
1. Click **Settings** in the left sidebar.
2. In the **CRM Configuration** card, click **Pipelines & Stages**.
3. Click **New pipeline** at the top right.
4. Type a name in **Pipeline name** (for example "Renewals").
5. In **Stages from**, choose a template. The text under it lists the stages it creates.
6. Click **Create pipeline**.

## Result
A "Pipeline created" message appears and the new pipeline is selected in the list on the left. Its stages appear in the table on the right, ready to edit. The first pipeline in a workspace becomes the default automatically.

## Tips
- Every template comes with a won stage and a lost stage. You can rename, add, reorder or archive stages afterwards.
- With more than one pipeline, the **Pipeline** page (wide screens) also offers **Add pipeline** at the bottom of the **Pipelines** column. It opens this same settings page.

## Troubleshooting
- **"A pipeline with this name already exists."** Pipeline names must be unique. Choose another name.
- **The pipeline limit is reached.** Archive a pipeline you no longer use, or check your plan under **Settings → Billing & Subscription**. Sub-pipelines do not count towards the limit.
