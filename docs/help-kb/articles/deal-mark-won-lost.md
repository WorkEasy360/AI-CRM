## Overview
A deal is closed by moving it to a Won or Lost stage. When you mark a deal as lost you can record why, which helps you analyse lost business later.

## Before you start
- You need `deals.change_stage`. A Sales Representative can close only their own deals.

## Steps
1. Click **Pipeline** in the left sidebar.
2. Click the deal's name on its card, then click **Open full deal**.
3. On the stage track under the deal header, click **Win** to mark the deal as won.
4. Or click **Lose** to mark it as lost.
5. For a lost deal, type a reason in **Lost reason (optional)**.
6. Click **Mark as lost**.

## Result
The deal's status changes to **Won** or **Lost** and the closing date is recorded. On the deal page, **Won on** or **Lost on** and the **Lost reason** appear in the details.

## Tips
- On the board you can also drag the card to the Won or Lost column; a lost move shows the same reason dialog.
- To reopen a closed deal, move it back to an open stage.
- The lost reason can be up to 255 characters.

## Troubleshooting
- **Win or Lose is greyed out.** Your role cannot change this deal's stage, or the deal is already won or lost.
- **No Win or Lose button.** The pipeline has no Won or Lost stage. Ask a Sales Manager or Admin to add one.
