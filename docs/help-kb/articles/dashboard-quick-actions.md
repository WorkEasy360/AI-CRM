## Overview
The **Quick Actions** panel on the Dashboard gives one-tap shortcuts to the things you do most often.

## Before you start
- Each action only appears if your role allows it. For example, Viewers do not see **Add Lead**.

## Steps
1. Click **Dashboard** in the left sidebar.
2. Find the **Quick Actions** panel next to **Leads by Status**.
3. Click **Add Lead**, **Add Task**, **Schedule Follow-up** or **Add Deal** to open the create form on this page.
4. Complete and save the form.
5. For more shortcuts, click **More insights** and use **More Actions**: **Add Contact**, **Add Deal**, **Add Task**, **Schedule Meeting**, **Send WhatsApp**, **Send Email**, **Create Automation** and **View Reports**.

## Result
The record is created, or you land on the right page to finish the task.

## Tips
- **Add Lead** creates a contact with the status Lead.
- The same create forms are available from **Create** in the top bar on every page.

## Troubleshooting
- **"Your role does not include access to this data."** shows instead of actions. Your role has none of these permissions. Ask an administrator.
