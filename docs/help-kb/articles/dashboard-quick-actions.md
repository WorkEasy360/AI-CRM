## Overview
The **Quick Actions** panel on the Dashboard gives one-click shortcuts to the things you do most often.

## Before you start
- Each tile only appears if your role allows that action. For example, Viewers do not see **Add Contact**.

## Steps
1. Click **Dashboard** in the left sidebar.
2. Scroll to the **Quick Actions** panel next to **Recent Activities**.
3. Click a tile:
   - **Add Contact**, **Add Deal**, **Add Task** or **Schedule Meeting** open the create form on this page.
   - **Send WhatsApp** opens the WhatsApp tab of Activities.
   - **Send Email** opens the Emails tab of Activities.
   - **Create Automation** opens **Settings → Automation**.
   - **View Reports** opens the Pipeline **Analytics** tab.
4. Complete and save the form, or continue on the page that opened.

## Result
The record is created, or you land on the right page to finish the task.

## Tips
- The same create forms are available from **Create** in the top bar on every page.

## Troubleshooting
- **"Your role does not include access to this data."** shows instead of tiles. Your role has none of these permissions. Ask an administrator.
