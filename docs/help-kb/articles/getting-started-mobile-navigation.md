## Overview
On phones and small tablets the CRM shows a bottom bar with the pages you use most, and the full sidebar moves into a slide-out menu.

## Steps
1. Use the bottom bar to jump to **Pipeline**, **Contacts** or **Activities**.
2. Tap **Search** to find any record.
3. Tap **New**, then choose **Task**, **Call**, **Contact** or **Deal** under **Create** to add a record.
4. For every other page (Dashboard, AI Assistant, Helpdesk, Products, Settings), tap **Open navigation** (the menu icon at the top left).
5. Tap the page you want. The menu closes automatically.

## Result
You can work with the full CRM on a small screen, with your most common tasks one tap away.

## Tips
- The **Ask WorkEasy AI** button floats just above the bottom bar, so the assistant is always within reach.
- The bottom bar only shows **Search** and **New** if your role can search and create records.
