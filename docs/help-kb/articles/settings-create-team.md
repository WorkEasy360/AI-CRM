## Overview
Teams group people, for example "North Sales". Roles that work on "Own team" records, such as Sales Representative, see the records of their team members.

## Before you start
- Only the Owner and Admins can create, edit or delete teams.

## Steps
1. Click **Settings** in the left sidebar.
2. In the **Workspace** card, click **Users & Teams**.
3. Click the **Teams** tab.
4. Click **New team**.
5. Enter a **Team name**.
6. Optionally choose a **Manager** (or leave **No manager**).
7. Click **Create team**.
8. If a **Confirm it's you** window appears, enter your password and click **Confirm**.

## Result
A message says **Team created** and the team appears as a card showing its member count and manager.

## Tips
- To rename a team or change its manager, click the **…** button on the team card, click **Edit**, change the details and click **Save changes**.
- To delete a team, click the **…** button, click **Delete**, then click **Delete team**. Members keep their access; only the grouping is removed.
- When there are no teams yet, you can also click **Create a team**.

## Troubleshooting
- **I do not see the New team button.** Your role cannot manage teams. Ask the Owner or an Admin.
