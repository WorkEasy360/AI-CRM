## Overview
Field permissions let you hide a field from a profile, or let it see the field but not change it. Use them for sensitive values such as discounts or prices.

## Before you start
- The Owner and Admins can change field permissions.
- The Owner profile always has full access and is not listed.

## Steps
1. Click **Settings** in the left sidebar.
2. In the **Workspace** card, click **Roles & Permissions**.
3. Click the **Field permissions** tab.
4. In the **Profile** drop-down, choose the profile to restrict.
5. In the **Module** drop-down, choose **Contacts**, **Companies**, **Deals**, **Deal product lines** or **Products**.
6. Next to each field, choose **Read & write**, **Read only** or **Hidden**.
7. Click **Save**.

## Result
A message says **Field permissions saved**. People with that profile no longer see hidden fields, and cannot edit read-only ones.

## Tips
- Custom fields are listed too, marked "(custom)".
- Name, owner, tags and record dates cannot be restricted.

## Troubleshooting
- **Save is disabled.** You have not changed any field yet.
