## Overview
The **Emails** tab on the Activities page lists the emails you can see: those sent from the CRM and replies synced from connected mailboxes, newest first.

## Before you start
- You need `email.view`. All built-in roles have it.
- Emails are sent and synced through a mailbox connected in **Settings** → **Communication** → **Email Settings**.

## Steps
1. Click **Activities** in the left sidebar.
2. Click the **Emails** tab.
3. Read the columns: **Activity** (subject, with a sent or received arrow), **Related to**, **Date**, **Owner** and **Status**.
4. Click **Show more** to load older emails.
5. To read or answer an email, click the contact's name in **Related to**.
6. On the contact's page, click the **Emails** tab.
7. Click **Reply** on the email, or **New email** to write a new one.

## Result
The contact's email thread opens, where you can read full messages and reply.

## Tips
- **Owner** shows who sent the email, or "Customer" for received emails.
- A red **failed** status means the email could not be sent.

## Troubleshooting
- **"No emails yet"** Nothing has been sent from the CRM yet, or no mailbox is connected.
- **No Emails tab?** Your role lacks `email.view`.
