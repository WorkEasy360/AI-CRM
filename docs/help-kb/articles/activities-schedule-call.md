## Overview
Schedule a call you plan to make, so it shows in your list, on the calendar and in **Today's Schedule** on the day.

## Before you start
- You need the `activities.create` permission (Owner, Admin, Sales Manager, Sales Representative).

## Steps
1. Click **Activities** in the left sidebar.
2. Click the **Calls** tab.
3. Click **New call**. (From any tab you can also click the arrow next to the main button and choose **Schedule call**.)
4. Enter a **Title**.
5. Set **When** to the date and time of the call.
6. Choose the **Direction**: **Outbound** or **Inbound**.
7. Optional: change **Duration (minutes)** (default 15).
8. Optional: type **Notes**.
9. Optional: click **More details** to link a **Contact**, **Company** or **Deal** and set a **Priority**.
10. Click **Schedule call**.

## Result
A "Call scheduled" message appears. The call is listed in the **Calls** tab and on the **Calendar**.

## Tips
- To record a call that already happened, use **Log a call** instead.
- When you later mark the call as done from the grouped list or **My Day**, you are asked how it went so the outcome is saved.
- Calls cannot repeat. For a regular check-in, schedule a repeating meeting or task.

## Troubleshooting
- **"Choose when the call happens."** The **When** field is required for calls.
- **"Enter 1 to 1440 minutes."** The duration must be a whole number of minutes, up to one day.
