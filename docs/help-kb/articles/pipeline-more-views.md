## Overview
Besides Kanban and List, deals can be shown as a **Sheet** (edit cells like a spreadsheet), a **Calendar** (deals placed on a date) or a **Chart** (deals grouped and counted or summed).

## Before you start
- To change cells in the Sheet you need permission to edit those deals (`deals.update`).

## Steps
1. Click **Pipeline** in the left sidebar.
2. Click **Filters**, then in the small view switcher click **Sheet**, **Calendar** or **Chart**.
3. In **Sheet**: click a cell and type, or copy, paste and fill down (Ctrl+D). Click **Columns** to add, remove or reorder columns.
4. In **Sheet**: click **Save … rows** to save your edits, or **Discard** to undo them.
5. In **Calendar**: use **Previous month** and **Next month**, and choose the date field in **Place by** (expected close by default).
6. In **Chart**: choose **Count records** or **Sum of a field**, then choose the field to group by. Click **Show table** to see the numbers.

## Result
The deals of the selected pipeline appear in the chosen view. Sheet edits are saved together when you click save.

## Tips
- In **Calendar**, the **My events, tasks and calls** switch shows your own activities next to the deals.
- Save a view you use often with **Saved views**.

## Troubleshooting
- **A cell is marked and not saved.** You may only view that field, or someone else changed the deal first. Hover the cell to see why.
