## Overview
Write and send an email to a contact from their record. Sent emails and replies on the same thread appear on the contact's **Emails** tab.

## Before you start
- Your mailbox must be connected in Settings → Communication → Email Settings.
- Owner, Admin, Sales Manager and Sales Representative can send email.

## Steps
1. Click **Contacts** in the left sidebar, then **People (Contacts)**.
2. Click the person's name to open the contact.
3. Click **Email** in the quick actions under the contact's details.
4. Check the address in **To**.
5. Enter a **Subject**.
6. Optionally choose a **Template**.
7. Write your text in **Message**.
8. Optionally click **Attach files**.
9. Click **Send**.

## Result
An "Email sent" (or "Email queued") message appears and the email is listed on the contact's **Emails** tab.

## Tips
- Click **Cc/Bcc** to add more recipients.
- On the **Emails** tab you can also click **New email**, or reply to a message in the thread.
- Company pages have no Email quick action; open the company's **Emails** tab and click **New email** instead.

## Troubleshooting
- **"Your mailbox is not connected"?** Click **Connect a mailbox in Settings → Email** in the message and connect your account.
