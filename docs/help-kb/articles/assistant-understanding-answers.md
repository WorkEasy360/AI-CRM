## Overview
Each answer separates real CRM figures from analysis and advice, so you always know what is a fact and what is a suggestion.

## Steps
1. Ask a question on the **AI Assistant** page or in the **Ask WorkEasy AI** panel.
2. Read the first line. It is the short answer.
3. Read **From your CRM**. These points are facts taken directly from your records.
4. Read **Analysis**, if shown. This is the assistant's interpretation of those facts.
5. Read **Suggested next step**, if shown. This is advice on what to do; nothing has been done for you.
6. Look at any extra sections, such as lists of deals or figures, for the records the answer is based on.
7. Under **Sources**, click a source that shows an arrow to open the record or conversation it came from.

## Result
You can check every figure against the record it came from before you act.

## Tips
- Treat **Analysis** and **Suggested next step** as advice and check the facts before acting.
- Answers only use records your role can see, so two people may get different answers to the same question.

## Troubleshooting
- **The answer has facts but no Analysis.** The assistant is in **Knowledge search mode**; see that article.
