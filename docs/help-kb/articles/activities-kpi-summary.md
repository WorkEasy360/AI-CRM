## Overview
The top of the Activities page shows four summary tiles, and the right side shows schedule cards. Counts include every activity you are allowed to see.

## Before you start
- You need `activities.view` (all built-in roles).

## Steps
1. Click **Activities** in the left sidebar.
2. Read the tiles at the top:
   - **Total Activities**: all tasks, calls and meetings.
   - **Tasks**: all tasks, with the number still open.
   - **Calls**: all calls, with the number this week.
   - **Meetings**: all meetings, with the number this week.
3. Read the percentage on each tile. It compares activities created in the last 30 days with the 30 days before.
4. On the right, check **Today's Schedule**, the month calendar, **Upcoming** and **Overdue**.
5. Click **View All** on **Upcoming** or **Overdue** to open the full list.

## Result
You see your workload at a glance and can jump straight to upcoming or late items.

## Tips
- **Upcoming** covers open activities due in the next seven days.
- Click a date in the month calendar to filter the list to that day.
