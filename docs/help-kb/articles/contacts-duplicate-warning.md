## Overview
While you type a new contact or company, WorkEasy checks for existing records that look the same and shows an amber "Possible duplicate" note. It is only a warning; it never stops you saving.

## Steps
1. Click **Contacts** in the left sidebar, then **People (Contacts)**.
2. Click **Add Contact**, then **Individual**.
3. Type the name, phone or email.
4. If a "Possible duplicate" note appears, click **Open** next to the match to check it in a new tab.
5. If it is the same person, click **Cancel** and update the existing contact instead.
6. If it is a different person, finish the form and save.

## Result
You avoid creating the same person twice, or you save a genuinely new contact.

## Tips
- Contacts are matched on email, phone, or full first and last name. Companies are matched on name or website.
- The check also runs in the **New contact**, **Edit contact**, **New company** and **Edit company** forms. When editing, it runs only after you change a name, email, phone or website.
- Only records you are allowed to see are checked.
