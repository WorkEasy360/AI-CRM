## Overview
Bulk actions let you mark many activities complete, or move them to another owner, in one step.

## Before you start
- You need the `activities.bulk_update` permission. Owner, Admin and Sales Manager have it; Sales Representative and Viewer do not.
- **Reassign** is shown only to roles that may assign activities to others (Owner, Admin, Sales Manager).

## Steps
1. Click **Activities** in the left sidebar.
2. Choose a tab: **All Activities**, **Tasks**, **Calls** or **Meetings**.
3. Click the **Grouped list** button next to the filters.
4. Click **Select**.
5. Tick the checkbox on each activity you want, or click **Select all** (shown with the number of loaded rows) to pick every loaded row.
6. Click **Mark complete**, or click **Reassign**.
7. If you chose **Reassign**, pick the **New owner** and click **Reassign**.

## Result
A message such as "5 of 5 updated" appears. Items that were already done, or already had that owner, are skipped.

## Tips
- Use the **View**, **Due** and search filters first so **Select all** picks only what you need.
- Click the **X** (**Stop selecting**) button to leave selection mode.
- Click **Table view** to return to the table.

## Troubleshooting
- **"Some selected activities are not yours to change. Nothing was updated."** Remove the activities you cannot edit and try again.
- **No Select button?** Your role does not have bulk actions.
