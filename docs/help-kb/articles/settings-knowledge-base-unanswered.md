## Overview
When WorkEasy AI has no guide for a question, it records the question for your admins. Members can also rate each answer. Use both to improve your help articles.

## Before you start
- You need the **Owner** or **Admin** role.

## Steps
1. Click **Settings** in the left sidebar.
2. In the **AI Assistant** card, click **Knowledge Base**.
3. Click the **Unanswered Questions** tab to see questions, how often they were asked and on which page.
4. Click **Create article** next to a question to write an answer, or **Dismiss** to hide it.
5. Click the **Feedback report** tab to see **Most asked**, **Lowest rated** and **Recent comments**.

## Result
A question you answer moves to **Answered**, and the new article is used for the same question from then on.

## Tips
- Choose **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days** or **Last 90 days** to change the report period.
