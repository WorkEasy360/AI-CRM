## Overview
Create an account to start your own CRM workspace. You only need your name, email and a password; there is no separate company setup step.

## Before you start
- If a colleague invited you to an existing workspace, use the invitation link in your email instead. Signing up here creates a new, separate workspace.

## Steps
1. On the **Sign in** page, click **Create account** (next to **New here?**).
2. Enter your **Name**.
3. Enter your **Email**.
4. Enter a **Password** of at least 12 characters. Avoid common passwords and your name or email.
5. Click **Create account**.
6. On the **Check your email** screen, note the address the verification link was sent to.
7. Open that email and click the verification link.

## Result
After you verify your email, your workspace is created for you, with you as its Owner and a ready-to-use pipeline.

## Tips
- Already have an account? Click **Sign in** at the bottom of the form.

## Troubleshooting
- **The sign-up page sends me back to Sign in.** Accounts on this installation come from Workeasy360, so sign-up is closed. Use **Access WorkEasy CRM** on the Sign in page instead.
- **"Could not create your account."** Read the message under the field it mentions, for example an email that is already registered.
