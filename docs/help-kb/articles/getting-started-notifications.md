## Overview
The bell in the top bar collects reminders and alerts: tasks due, upcoming meetings and calls, deals assigned to you, inactive deals, customer replies, high-risk deals, integration alerts and automation messages.

## Steps
1. Look at the bell icon in the top bar. A red dot means you have unread notifications.
2. Click the bell to open **Notifications**.
3. Click a notification to open the record it is about. It is marked as read.
4. To clear the red dot, click **Mark all read**.
5. To choose which notifications you receive, click **Preferences** at the bottom of the list.

## Result
Unread items are shown in bold with a dot. When nothing is waiting you see **You're all caught up**.

## Tips
- The unread count refreshes about once a minute, and when you return to the browser tab.
- **Preferences** opens **Settings → Notifications**.

## Troubleshooting
- **"Notifications are unavailable right now."** Close the menu and open it again in a moment.
- **Clicking a notification does nothing.** Some notifications are not linked to a record; they are only marked as read.
