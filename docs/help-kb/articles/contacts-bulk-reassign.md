## Overview
Hand a group of contacts or companies to another team member, for example when someone leaves or territories change.

## Before you start
- You need bulk update permission and permission to edit all contacts. Owner, Admin and Sales Manager have both.

## Steps
1. Click **Contacts** in the left sidebar, then **People (Contacts)**.
2. Click the **Individuals** tab.
3. Tick the checkboxes of the contacts you want to move.
4. In the selection bar, click **Reassign**.
5. In **New owner**, choose the team member.
6. Click **Reassign**.

## Result
A message such as "8 of 8 updated" appears and the **Owner** column shows the new owner.

## Tips
- Use the view menu (**My contacts**) or **Filters** first to narrow the list before selecting.
- The **Companies** list has the same **Reassign** button.

## Troubleshooting
- **No Reassign button?** Your role can edit only its own or its team's records, so it cannot reassign.
