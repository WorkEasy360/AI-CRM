## Overview
The Help Center lists every WorkEasy CRM guide, grouped by module. WorkEasy AI answers from these same guides.

## Steps
1. Click the **?** icon (**Help**) in the top bar.
2. Click **Help Center**.
3. Type in the search box, or click a module such as **Pipeline** or **Settings** to filter.
4. Click a guide to open it.
5. Click **Take me there** to open the screen the guide describes.

## Result
The guide shows the steps, what you will see when done, tips and related topics.

## Tips
- A lock next to a guide means your role does not include that feature.
- Click **Ask WorkEasy AI about this** on a guide to ask a follow-up question.
