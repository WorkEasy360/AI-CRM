## Overview
Each deal card on the Kanban board always shows the deal name, company or contact, amount, probability, owner and expected close date. You can add up to 8 extra deal fields, including custom fields, per pipeline.

## Before you start
- You need `pipelines.manage` (Owner, Admin or Sales Manager).
- Sub-pipelines use their parent pipeline's card layout.

## Steps
1. Click **Settings** in the left sidebar.
2. In the **CRM Configuration** card, click **Pipelines & Stages**.
3. In the list on the left, click the pipeline.
4. Scroll to the **Card layout** section below the stages.
5. Click the field chips you want to show. Selected chips are highlighted; click again to remove one.
6. Click **Save layout**.

## Result
"Card layout saved" appears. The chosen fields show on every card of that pipeline, each with its label.

## Tips
- After 8 fields are selected, the other chips are disabled. Remove one to pick another.
- A field with no value on a deal is not shown on that card.
