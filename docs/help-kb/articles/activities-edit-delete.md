## Overview
Edit an activity to change its title, details, status or linked records. Delete it to remove it for everyone.

## Before you start
- Editing needs `activities.update` and deleting needs `activities.delete` for the activity. A Sales Representative can edit and delete their own activities; Owner, Admin and Sales Manager can change anyone's. Viewer can only view.

## Steps
1. Click **Activities** in the left sidebar.
2. Click the **⋮** actions button at the end of the activity's row.
3. To edit, click **Edit**.
4. Change the fields you need. Click **More details** for **Description**, **Contact**, **Company**, **Deal**, **Owner** and **Status**.
5. Click **Save changes**.
6. To delete instead, open the **⋮** menu again and click **Delete**.
7. In **Delete this activity?**, click **Delete**.

## Result
After saving you see an "updated" message. After deleting you see "Activity deleted" and the item is removed from lists, the calendar and the record's timeline.

## Tips
- Clicking the activity's title in the table also opens the edit form.
- Set **Status** to **Cancelled** to keep a record of an activity that will not happen, instead of deleting it.
- **Owner** is shown only to roles that can assign activities to others (Owner, Admin, Sales Manager).
- Deleting a repeating activity removes every occurrence.

## Troubleshooting
- **You see View instead of Edit?** You can only view this activity.
