## Overview
A price book holds one price per product. Products without a price in the book use their own list price.

## Before you start
- You need `pricebooks.manage` (Owner, Admin or Sales Manager). Other roles can see the prices but not change them.

## Steps
1. Click **Settings** in the left sidebar.
2. In the **Also in Settings** links, click **Price books**.
3. Click **Prices** on the price book's row.
4. In **Find product**, type a name or SKU.
5. Choose the product in **Product**.
6. Enter the **Price** in the book's currency.
7. Click **Add**.

## Result
The product appears in the **Prices in** card with its **List price** and **Price in book**.

## Tips
- To change a price, type the new value in **Price in book** and click **Save** next to it.
- To remove a product from the book, click the **X** button on its row (labelled "Remove" followed by the product name).
- Only active products appear in **Product**, and products already in the book are left out.

## Troubleshooting
- **"Enter a price like 99 or 99.50."** Use digits with up to two decimals, no currency symbol.
