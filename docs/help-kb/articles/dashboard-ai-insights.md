## Overview
The **WorkEasy AI Insights** card under **More insights** on the Dashboard lists the few things that need your attention now. Each line is counted from CRM records you can see.

## Steps
1. Click **Dashboard** in the left sidebar.
2. Click **More insights** at the bottom and find the **WorkEasy AI Insights** card, next to **Upcoming & Overdue**.
3. Read the insights. They can include:
   - follow-ups due today,
   - deals close to winning (60% probability or more, closing within 30 days),
   - a change in your win rate,
   - open deals with no next step planned,
   - support tickets past their SLA.
4. Click an insight to open the list behind it.
5. To ask a question, click **Ask WorkEasy AI** at the bottom of the card.

## Result
You open the exact records to act on, or the AI Assistant page.

## Tips
- Insights only appear when there is something to act on.

## Troubleshooting
- **"All caught up: nothing needs your attention right now."** There is nothing to flag for this period. Try another period in the **Period** menu.
