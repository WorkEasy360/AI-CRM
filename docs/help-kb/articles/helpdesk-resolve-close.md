## Overview
A ticket moves through **Open**, **In Progress**, **Resolved** and **Closed**. It becomes **In Progress** automatically when someone first replies. You resolve, close or reopen it yourself.

## Before you start
- You need `tickets.update` for the ticket. Owner, Admin and Sales Manager can change any ticket; a Sales Representative can change tickets assigned to them.

## Steps
1. Click **Helpdesk** in the left sidebar.
2. Click the ticket's ID or subject to open **Ticket Details**.
3. Click **Mark as Resolved** at the bottom of the panel.
4. To close it instead, click **More** and then **Close**.
5. To open a resolved or closed ticket again, click **More** and then **Reopen**.

## Result
The status badge changes. Resolved and closed tickets are counted under the **Resolved** tab and no longer become overdue. A reopened ticket returns to **Open**.

## Tips
- To resolve several tickets at once, tick their checkboxes in the list and click **Mark as Resolved** in the bar that appears.
- From the list, the **⋮** actions button on a row also offers **Mark as Resolved**.

## Troubleshooting
- **Mark as Resolved is greyed out?** The ticket is already resolved or closed.
- **No buttons at the bottom of the panel?** Your role can only view this ticket.
