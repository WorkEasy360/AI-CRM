## Overview
The left sidebar takes you to every part of the CRM, and the top bar holds search, the create menu, notifications, help and your account menu.

## Steps
1. Look at the left sidebar. It lists **Dashboard**, **Pipeline**, **Contacts**, **Activities**, **AI Assistant**, **Helpdesk**, **Products** and **Settings**.
2. Click any item to open that page. The current page is highlighted.
3. To reach people or companies, click **Contacts**, then choose **People (Contacts)** or **Companies** underneath it.
4. To hide or show those two sub-items, click the arrow next to **Contacts** (**Collapse Contacts** / **Expand Contacts**).
5. To return to the Dashboard from anywhere, click your workspace name and logo at the top of the sidebar.
6. Use the top bar on the right for **Create**, **Notifications**, **Help** (the **?** icon) and the **Account menu** (your avatar).

## Result
You can reach every page in one or two clicks. The sidebar also shows the **WorkEasy AI** card with an **Ask AI Now** button that opens the AI Assistant.

## Tips
- Press **Ctrl K** (or **⌘ K** on a Mac) to search instead of browsing.
- If your role can use WhatsApp, a green **WhatsApp inbox** button in the top bar opens the WhatsApp tab of Activities.
- Administrative pages are inside **Settings**, not in the sidebar.

## Troubleshooting
- **I cannot see the sidebar.** On a small screen it is hidden. Tap **Open navigation** (the menu icon at the top left) to open it.
