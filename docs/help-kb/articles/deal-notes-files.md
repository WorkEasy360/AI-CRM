## Overview
Notes keep the story of a deal in one place, and files keep proposals, contracts and other documents with the deal.

## Before you start
- Adding notes needs `notes.create`; uploading files needs `files.upload`. Owner, Admin, Sales Manager and Sales Representative have both; Viewer can only read them.

## Steps
1. Click **Pipeline** in the left sidebar.
2. Click the deal's name on its card to open the **Deal Details** panel.
3. Click the **Notes** tab.
4. Type in **Write a note… (plain text)** and click **Add note**.
5. Click the **Files** tab.
6. Drop a file onto the upload box, or click **Choose a file** and pick it.

## Result
The note appears in the list with its author and time. The file appears in the files list, where it can be downloaded.

## Tips
- The same **Notes** and **Files** tabs are on the full deal page (**Open full deal**).
- Files can be PDF, image, text or Office documents, up to 10 MB each.
- Use the pencil and bin buttons on a note to edit or delete it. You can change your own notes; managers can change all.

## Troubleshooting
- **The upload box is missing.** Your role cannot upload files, or the deal is deleted.
