## Overview
A meeting is a calendar event with a start and end time, team attendees, a location or meeting link, and an optional reminder.

## Before you start
- You need the `activities.create` permission (Owner, Admin, Sales Manager, Sales Representative).

## Steps
1. Click **Activities** in the left sidebar.
2. Click the **Meetings** tab.
3. Click **New meeting**.
4. Enter a **Title**.
5. Set **Starts** and **Ends**. For a full-day event, turn on **All day** and pick a **Date** instead.
6. Optional: add team members under **Attendees**.
7. Optional: enter a **Location** or a **Meeting link**.
8. Optional: choose a **Reminder** (default **30 minutes before**).
9. Optional: set **Repeat** for a recurring meeting.
10. Optional: click **More details** to add a **Description**, link a **Contact**, **Company** or **Deal**, or set a **Priority**.
11. Click **Schedule meeting**.

## Result
A "Meeting scheduled" message appears. The meeting shows on the **Calendar** and in the **Meetings** tab.

## Tips
- On the **Calendar** tab, click the date of an empty day (month view) or double-click a day column (week or day view) to start a meeting on that day.
- The meeting link must start with http:// or https://.

## Troubleshooting
- **"The meeting must end after it starts."** Set **Ends** later than **Starts**.
- **"Choose when the meeting starts."** **Starts** is required.
