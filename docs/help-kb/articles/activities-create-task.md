## Overview
A task is a to-do with an optional due date and priority. Link it to a contact, company or deal so the next step is never forgotten.

## Before you start
- You need the `activities.create` permission. Owner, Admin, Sales Manager and Sales Representative have it; Viewer does not.

## Steps
1. Click **Activities** in the left sidebar.
2. Click **New task** at the top right. (On another tab, click the arrow button next to the main button and choose **Task**.)
3. Type a **Title**, for example "Send the revised quote".
4. Optional: pick a **Due date** and a **Time**.
5. Optional: choose a **Priority** (**Low**, **Normal**, **High** or **Urgent**).
6. Optional: click **More details** to add a **Description** or link a **Contact**, **Company** or **Deal**.
7. Click **Create task**.

## Result
A "Task created" message appears and the task shows in the **All Activities** and **Tasks** tabs. A task with a due date also appears in **My Day** on that day, and in the **Overdue** card once it is late.

## Tips
- A title is enough. You can add the due date later with **Edit**.
- You can also create a task from any page with the **Create** button in the top bar, then **Task**.
- Turn on **Mark as done** under **More details** to record a task you already finished.

## Troubleshooting
- **"Pick a date for that time."** You entered a **Time** without a **Due date**. Pick a date or clear the time.
- **No New task button?** Your role cannot create activities. Ask an Admin to check your role under **Roles & Permissions**.
