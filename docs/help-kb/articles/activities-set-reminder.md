## Overview
A meeting can send you a notification before it starts. You set the reminder on the meeting form.

## Before you start
- To add a reminder to a new meeting you need `activities.create`. To change it on an existing meeting you need edit rights on that meeting (`activities.update`).
- Reminders are available for meetings only. The task and call forms have no reminder field.

## Steps
1. Click **Activities** in the left sidebar.
2. Click the **Meetings** tab.
3. Click **New meeting**, or click an existing meeting's title to edit it.
4. Open the **Reminder** list.
5. Choose **10 minutes before**, **30 minutes before**, **1 hour before** or **1 day before**. Choose **No reminder** to turn it off.
6. Click **Schedule meeting** for a new meeting, or **Save changes** for an existing one.

## Result
You get a notification at the chosen time before the meeting, as long as the meeting is still open.

## Tips
- New meetings default to **30 minutes before**.
- If you move the meeting, the reminder moves with it.
- Logged (past) meetings and calls never send reminders.
