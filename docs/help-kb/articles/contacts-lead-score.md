## Overview
The lead score is a number from 0 to 100 shown next to a contact's status. It is a rules-based score, not a prediction: it adds and removes points for facts about the contact.

## Steps
1. Click **Contacts** in the left sidebar, then **People (Contacts)**.
2. Click the person's name to open the contact.
3. Click the score chip next to the status badge under the name.
4. Read the reasons listed under **Rules-based score**.

## Result
A small panel shows the score out of 100 and the main reasons that moved it.

## Tips
- Points go up for: a later status (Prospect, Qualified, Customer), a complete profile (email, phone, company, job title), open or won deals, recent activity, a scheduled next activity, meetings, connected calls, email or WhatsApp replies, a larger company, and referral or website sources.
- Points go down for: Inactive status, a mostly empty profile, no activity for over 60 days, or never being contacted.
- Green means 70 or more, amber 40 to 69.

## Troubleshooting
- **"Nothing has moved this score yet."** Log activities and link deals to raise it.
