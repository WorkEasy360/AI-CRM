## Overview
Tasks and meetings can repeat daily, weekly, monthly or yearly. Calls cannot repeat.

## Before you start
- You need `activities.create` for a new activity, or `activities.update` to change an existing one.
- A task must have a **Due date** before it can repeat.

## Steps
1. Click **Activities** in the left sidebar.
2. Click **New task** (or open the arrow menu and choose **Meeting**), or click an existing task or meeting to edit it.
3. Fill in the **Title** and the **Due date** (task) or **Starts** (meeting).
4. Open the **Repeat** list and choose **Daily**, **Weekly**, **Monthly** or **Yearly**.
5. In **Every**, enter how often, for example 2 for every two weeks.
6. For **Weekly**, click the weekday buttons to pick the days.
7. In the end list, choose **Never ends**, **Ends after** (then enter the number of times) or **Ends on** (then pick an end date).
8. Click **Create task**, **Schedule meeting** or **Save changes**.

## Result
The activity shows a repeat icon with a summary such as "Weekly on Mon, Wed". A repeating meeting appears on every matching day of the **Calendar**. A repeating task creates its next occurrence each time you complete it.

## Tips
- To stop a series, edit it and set **Repeat** to **Does not repeat**.
- Clicking one occurrence of a repeating meeting opens the meeting itself, so changes apply to the whole series.

## Troubleshooting
- **"Pick a due date to repeat this task."** Add a **Due date** first.
- **"Choose an end count or an end date, not both."** Pick only one end option.
