## Overview
The Helpdesk list shows tickets with their ID, subject, customer, category, priority, status, agent and last update. Tabs, search and filters narrow it.

## Before you start
- You need `tickets.view`. All built-in roles have it. A Sales Representative sees their own and their team's tickets.

## Steps
1. Click **Helpdesk** in the left sidebar.
2. Click a tab: **All Tickets**, **Open**, **In Progress**, **Resolved** or **Overdue**. Each tab shows its count.
3. Type in **Search tickets by ID, subject, customer…**.
4. Optional: choose a priority in **All Priorities**.
5. Optional: choose a category in **All Categories**.
6. Optional: choose an agent in **All Agents**, or **Me**.
7. Optional: choose a product in **All Products**.

## Result
The list shows only matching tickets. Click **Load more** at the bottom to see more. Click a ticket's ID or subject to open **Ticket Details**.

## Tips
- The **Resolved** tab includes closed tickets.
- The tiles at the top (**Open**, **In Progress**, **Resolved**, **Overdue**) also open their tab.
- **All Products** appears only if your role can view products.

## Troubleshooting
- **"No tickets match these filters"** Switch to **All Tickets**, reset each filter to its "All" option and clear the search.
- **"Helpdesk is not part of your role"** Ask an Admin to give your role access to tickets.
