## Overview
Logging a call records a conversation that already took place, with its outcome and notes. The call is saved as completed.

## Before you start
- You need the `activities.create` permission (Owner, Admin, Sales Manager, Sales Representative).

## Steps
1. Click **Activities** in the left sidebar.
2. Click the arrow button next to the main create button (**More ways to add**).
3. Click **Log a call**.
4. Enter a **Title**.
5. Check **When** (it defaults to now) and choose the **Direction**.
6. Optional: set **Duration (minutes)**.
7. Choose an **Outcome**, for example **Connected**, **No answer**, **Voicemail**, **Interested** or **Follow-up required**.
8. Optional: type **Notes** about what was discussed.
9. Optional: click **More details** and link a **Contact**, **Company** or **Deal**.
10. Click **Log call**.

## Result
A "Call logged" message appears. The call is saved as **Completed** and shows on the linked record's timeline.

## Tips
- On a contact or deal page, the **Call** quick action opens the same log form with the record already linked.
- An outcome of **Follow-up required** keeps the call in the **Follow-ups** tab until it is completed.
