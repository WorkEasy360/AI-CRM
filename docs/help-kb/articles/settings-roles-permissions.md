## Overview
Every member has one role (called a profile on this page). WorkEasy has five built-in roles:
- **Owner**: full control, including billing and the organization itself.
- **Admin**: full data, user and settings management, but no billing.
- **Sales Manager**: works with all contacts, companies, deals, activities and tickets, including import and export; manages pipelines, price books and tags; shares views and dashboards; sees and exports reports; sends email and WhatsApp. Cannot manage users, roles, custom fields or the audit log.
- **Sales Representative**: sees their team's contacts, companies, deals, activities and tickets; creates new ones; edits, deletes and moves only their own; sends email and WhatsApp; sees team reports.
- **Viewer**: read-only access to records, reports and tickets.

## Before you start
- The Owner and Admins can open this page.

## Steps
1. Click **Settings** in the left sidebar.
2. In the **Workspace** card, click **Roles & Permissions**.
3. On the **Profiles** tab, click a role in the list.
4. Read each permission and its access level on the right.

## Result
You see exactly what the role allows. Access levels are **No access**, **Own records**, **Own + subordinates**, **Own team** and **All records**.

## Tips
- Built-in roles are marked **System** and cannot be changed. Create a custom profile from one to adjust it.
- The number next to each role is how many people have it.

## Troubleshooting
- **A colleague cannot see a record.** Check their role's access level here, and their team or place in the **Role hierarchy**.
