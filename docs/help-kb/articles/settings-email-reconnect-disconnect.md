## Overview
If your mailbox shows **Needs attention** or **Disconnected**, reconnect it to keep sending email from the CRM. Disconnect it when you no longer want the CRM to use that account.

## Before you start
- You need the Owner, Admin, Sales Manager or Sales Representative role (`email.connect`).

## Steps
1. Click **Settings** in the left sidebar.
2. In the **Communication** card, click **Email Settings**.
3. In the **Connected mailbox** card, find your mailbox and check its badge: **Connected**, **Needs attention** or **Disconnected**.
4. To fix a mailbox that is not connected, click **Reconnect** and sign in to the provider again.
5. To remove a mailbox, click **Disconnect**.
6. In the confirmation, click **Disconnect**.

## Result
After reconnecting you see "Mailbox connected" and the badge changes to **Connected**. After disconnecting you see "Mailbox disconnected".

## Tips
- Messages already logged stay on their records after you disconnect.
- To switch to a different address, disconnect the old mailbox, then use **Connect Gmail** or **Connect Microsoft 365** again.

## Troubleshooting
- **Red error text under the mailbox?** It shows the provider's reason. Reconnect; if it keeps failing, ask an administrator to check the OAuth settings.
- **You cannot send email after disconnecting.** Connect a mailbox again first.
