## Overview
Change your password from the **Security** page whenever you think it may be known to someone else. Choose a strong password you do not use anywhere else.

## Before you start
- You need your current password. If you have forgotten it, sign out and use **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page.

## Steps
1. Click **Settings** in the left sidebar.
2. In the **Security & Data** card, click **Password & 2FA**.
3. In the **Password** card, enter your **Current password**.
4. Enter a **New password** of at least 12 characters.
5. Enter it again in **Confirm new password**.
6. Click **Update password**.
7. If a **Confirm it's you** window appears, enter your password and click **Confirm**.

## Result
A message says **Password changed**. Use the new password the next time you sign in.

## Tips
- You can also reach this page from the **Account menu** (your avatar, top right) → **Profile & security**.
- After a change, check **Active sessions** on the same page and sign out any device you do not recognise.

## Troubleshooting
- **"Passwords do not match."** Type the same new password in both boxes.
- **There is no Password card.** You sign in with your Workeasy360 account. Change your password in Workeasy360 instead.
- **The new password is rejected.** Avoid common passwords and your name or email.
