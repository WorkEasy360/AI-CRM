## Overview
A price book is a named list of special prices for your products, in one currency. Use one for a customer group, a region or a currency. On a deal you can pick a price book so its prices are used instead of list prices.

## Before you start
- You need `pricebooks.manage`. Owner, Admin and Sales Manager have it.
- Add your products first.

## Steps
1. Click **Settings** in the left sidebar.
2. In the **Also in Settings** links under the cards, click **Price books**.
3. Click **New price book**.
4. Type a **Name**, for example "Wholesale".
5. Enter the **Currency** as a 3-letter code, for example INR.
6. Optionally type a **Description**.
7. Click **Create price book**.

## Result
"Price book created" appears and the book is listed with its currency, product count and **Active** status. Next, add prices to it.

## Tips
- The currency cannot be changed later. Create another price book for another currency.
- A deal can only use a price book in the deal's own currency.

## Troubleshooting
- **An error on Name.** Another price book already has this name.
