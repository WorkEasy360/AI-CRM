## Overview
The **Active sessions** card lists every browser and device signed in to your account, with its IP address and sign-in time. Sign out any you do not recognise.

## Steps
1. Click **Settings** in the left sidebar.
2. In the **Security & Data** card, click **Password & 2FA**.
3. Scroll to the **Active sessions** card.
4. Click **Sign out** next to a device, or click **Sign out other sessions** to sign out everything except this device.
5. If a **Confirm it's you** window appears, enter your password and click **Confirm**.

## Result
A message says **Session signed out** or **Other sessions signed out**, and those devices disappear from the list.

## Tips
- The device you are using is marked **This device** and cannot be signed out here.
- If you see a device you do not know, change your password and turn on two-factor authentication.
- An Owner or Admin can sign out another member everywhere with **Revoke sessions** on **Users & Teams**.
