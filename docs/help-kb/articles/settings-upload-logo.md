## Overview
Your workspace logo appears in the sidebar for everyone in the workspace. Without one, the WorkEasy360 logo is shown.

## Before you start
- Only the Owner and Admins can change the logo.
- Use a PNG, JPEG or WebP image of at most 512 KB.

## Steps
1. Click **Settings** in the left sidebar.
2. In the **Workspace** card, click **Organization Details**.
3. Scroll to the **Logo** card.
4. Click **Upload logo**.
5. Choose the image file on your computer.
6. If a **Confirm it's you** window appears, enter your password and click **Confirm**.

## Result
A message says **Logo updated** and the new logo appears in the sidebar.

## Tips
- To go back to the default logo, click **Remove** in the **Logo** card. A message says **Logo removed**.
- To change the name next to the logo, edit **CRM name** in the **General** card.

## Troubleshooting
- **"That image is too large".** Choose an image of at most 512 KB.
- **"Could not change the logo".** Check that the file really is a PNG, JPEG or WebP image; the server checks the file contents.
