## Overview
**My Day** lists everything on your plate today: overdue items, today's tasks, events and calls, and tasks without a date. **Today's Schedule** on the right shows today's timed items as a timeline.

## Before you start
- You need `activities.view` (all built-in roles). My Day shows only your own activities.

## Steps
1. Click **Activities** in the left sidebar.
2. Click the **My Day** tab.
3. Review the sections: **Overdue**, **Tasks**, **Events**, **Calls** and **No date**.
4. Tick the checkbox on a row to mark it as done.
5. Use the **⋯** actions button on a row to **Edit** it or add a subtask.

## Result
The header shows today's date and how many items you have **completed today**. Finished items leave the open sections.

## Tips
- The **Today's Schedule** card on the right side of the Activities page is always visible. Click an item there to open it.
- If nothing is planned, click **New task** in the empty My Day view to add one.
- The dashboard's **Today's Follow-ups** panel links here with **View All**.

## Troubleshooting
- **"Could not load your day"** Click **Try again**.
- **"Nothing scheduled for today."** in Today's Schedule means no item today has a time. Undated tasks show only in **My Day**.
