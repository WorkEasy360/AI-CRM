## Overview
Internal notes let your team share context on a ticket. They are visible to your team only and are never sent to the customer.

## Before you start
- You need `tickets.update` for the ticket. Owner, Admin and Sales Manager can add notes to any ticket; a Sales Representative can add notes to tickets assigned to them.

## Steps
1. Click **Helpdesk** in the left sidebar.
2. Click the ticket's ID or subject to open **Ticket Details**.
3. Click the **Internal Notes** tab.
4. Type your note in **Add an internal note…**.
5. Click the **Add note** button.

## Result
The note appears in the **Internal Notes** tab with a yellow background, your name and the time. The ticket's **Activity** tab records that a note was added.

## Tips
- Use notes to hand over a ticket before you reassign it.
- Notes do not change the ticket's status.
