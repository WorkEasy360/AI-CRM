## Overview
If you cannot remember your password, request a reset link by email and choose a new password.

## Steps
1. On the **Sign in** page, click **Forgot password?**.
2. On **Reset your password**, enter your **Email**.
3. Click **Send reset link**.
4. Open the reset email and click the link.
5. On **Choose a new password**, enter a **New password** of at least 12 characters.
6. Enter the same password in **Confirm new password**.
7. Click **Update password**.
8. On **Password updated**, click **Sign in** and sign in with the new password.

## Result
Your password is changed and you can sign in with it.

## Tips
- For security, the confirmation always says **If an account exists for this email, password reset instructions have been sent.**, even when the email is not registered.
- Already signed in? Change your password from **Account menu → Profile & security** instead.

## Troubleshooting
- **"This reset link is invalid or has expired."** Request a new link with **Forgot password?**.
- **"Passwords do not match."** Type the same password in both fields.
- **The page sends me back to Sign in.** You sign in with Workeasy360, so passwords are reset in Workeasy360.
