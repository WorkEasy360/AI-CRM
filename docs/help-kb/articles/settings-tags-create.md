## Overview
Tags label contacts, companies, deals and products so you can filter and group them. Tags are shared across the whole organization.

## Before you start
- You need the Owner, Admin or Sales Manager role (`tags.manage`).

## Steps
1. Click **Settings** in the left sidebar.
2. In the **CRM Configuration** card, click **Tags**.
3. Click **New tag**.
4. Type a **Name**, for example "VIP" (up to 40 characters).
5. Under **Colour**, click a colour: Slate, Blue, Teal, Green, Amber, Red, Purple or Pink.
6. Check the **Preview** chip.
7. Click **Create tag**.

## Result
A "Tag created" message appears. The tag is listed with its **Usage** (number of records), **Colour** and **Created** date, and can now be applied to any record.

## Tips
- Tag names are unique within the organization, ignoring upper and lower case.
- On the Contacts page you can show only the contacts that carry a given tag.

## Troubleshooting
- **"A tag with this name already exists."** Choose a different name, or use the existing tag.
- **No New tag button?** Your role cannot manage tags. Ask an Owner, Admin or Sales Manager.
