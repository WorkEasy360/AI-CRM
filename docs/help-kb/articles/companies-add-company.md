## Overview
Add a business or organization so its people, deals and activities are grouped under one company record.

## Before you start
- You need permission to create companies. Owner, Admin, Sales Manager and Sales Representative can; Viewer cannot.

## Steps
1. Click **Contacts** in the left sidebar, then **Companies**.
2. Click **Add Contact**.
3. Choose **Company**.
4. Enter the **Company Name**.
5. Optionally enter the **Phone** (with country code) and **Website**.
6. Optionally add **Tags** and **Notes**.
7. Click **Save Company**.

## Result
A "Company saved" message appears and the company is listed on the **Companies** tab and in **All Contacts**.

## Tips
- For more fields (Industry, Status, Company size, Annual revenue, Owner, address), click **Company** in the Companies toolbar instead. That opens the **New company** form; click **Create company** to save.
- You can also create a company while adding a person: type its name in **Company Name (Optional)** and choose **Create company**.
- To import many companies, open **Add Contact** → **Import**.

## Troubleshooting
- **"Possible duplicate" warning in the New company form?** A company with the same name or website already exists. Click **Open** to check it before saving.
