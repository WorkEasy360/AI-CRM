## Overview
The Kanban view shows the selected pipeline as columns, one per stage, with a card for each deal. It is the default view of the **Pipeline** page.

## Steps
1. Click **Pipeline** in the left sidebar.
2. Click **Kanban View** in the view tabs.
3. Read each column header: the stage name, the number of deals, the total amount and, for open stages, the **Weighted** amount.
4. Read a card: deal name, company or contact, amount, probability, priority, owner, expected close date and the next activity.
5. Click a deal name to open the **Deal Details** panel.

## Result
You see where every deal stands and what each stage is worth.

## Tips
- A card with **No next step** has no scheduled activity. Plan one so the deal does not stall.
- A red close date means the expected close date has passed.
- Columns load up to 200 cards at first. Click **Show … more** at the bottom of a column to load the rest.
- Use the **Sort** drop-down to order cards by **Recently moved**, **Amount high–low**, **Closing soonest** or **Name A–Z**.
- The five tiles above the tabs show **Total Deals**, **Pipeline Value**, **Expected Revenue**, **Win Rate** and **Avg. Deal Cycle**, compared with the previous 30 days.

## Troubleshooting
- **"This pipeline has no active stages yet."** Add stages under **Settings → CRM Configuration → Pipelines & Stages**.
