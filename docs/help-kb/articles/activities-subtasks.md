## Overview
Subtasks split a task into smaller steps you can tick off one by one. The task shows progress such as 2/5.

## Before you start
- You need `activities.create` and edit rights on the parent task (`activities.update`).
- Only tasks can have subtasks, one level deep, up to 50 per task.

## Steps
1. Click **Activities** in the left sidebar.
2. Click the **Tasks** tab.
3. Click the **Grouped list** button next to the search and filters.
4. Click the **⋯** actions button on the task's row.
5. Click **Add subtask**.
6. Type the step in **Add a subtask**.
7. Click **Add** (or press Enter). Repeat for more steps.

## Result
The subtasks appear under the task. A counter chip, for example 0/3, shows how many are done. Click the chip to show or hide the subtasks.

## Tips
- Tick a subtask's checkbox to mark it done; tick it again to reopen it.
- Subtasks also work in the **Activities** tab of a contact, company or deal.
- Click **Table view** to go back to the table.

## Troubleshooting
- **No Add subtask option?** It is shown only for top-level tasks you can edit. Calls, meetings and subtasks cannot have subtasks.
- **The add box disappeared?** The task already has 50 subtasks.
