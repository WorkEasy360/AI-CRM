## Overview
Companies are archived, not permanently deleted. An archived company is hidden from lists and searches and can be restored later.

## Before you start
- Sales Representatives can archive companies they own. Owner, Admin and Sales Manager can archive any company.

## Steps
1. Click **Contacts** in the left sidebar, then **Companies**.
2. Click the ⋮ button at the end of the company's row.
3. Click **Archive**.

## Result
An "Archived" message appears and the company leaves the list.

## Tips
- To restore: open the view menu at the top left of the list, choose **Archived companies**, click the ⋮ button on the row and choose **Restore**.
- On the company page, the **More actions** button next to **Edit** has the same **Archive** and **Restore** options.
- Archiving a company does not archive its contacts or deals.

## Troubleshooting
- **No ⋮ button on the row?** You cannot edit or archive that company.
