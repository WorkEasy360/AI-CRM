## Overview
Invite colleagues by email. They receive a secure link, create their own password and join your workspace with the role you choose. Administrators never see or set anyone's password.

## Before you start
- Only the Owner and Admins can invite users.
- Decide which role the person needs (see **Roles & Permissions**). The Owner role cannot be given by invitation.

## Steps
1. Click **Settings** in the left sidebar.
2. In the **Workspace** card, click **Users & Teams**.
3. Click **Invite user**.
4. Enter the person's **Name**.
5. Enter their **Email**.
6. Under **Role**, click **Choose a role** and pick a role.
7. Optionally pick a team under **Team (optional)**.
8. Click **Send invitation**.
9. If a **Confirm it's you** window appears, enter your password and click **Confirm**.

## Result
A message says **Invitation sent**. The person appears in the list with the status **Invited** and an expiry date. The link works for 7 days.

## Tips
- **Team (optional)** only appears once at least one team exists.
- Inviting someone as Admin counts as a sensitive action, so you may be asked for your password.

## Troubleshooting
- **"This person is already a member of the organization."** They are already in the list; change their role instead.
- **"Your plan's user limit has been reached."** Remove a user or raise your licences in **Billing & Subscription**.
- **They did not get the email.** Use **Resend invitation** from the row's actions menu.
