## Overview
Products are the items and services you sell, with their price and tax. Once a product exists, it can be added to deals as a line item.

## Before you start
- You need `products.create`. Owner and Admin have it; Sales Manager, Sales Representative and Viewer can only view products.

## Steps
1. Click **Products** in the left sidebar.
2. Click **Product** (with the plus icon) at the right of the toolbar. On an empty catalogue, click **Add product**.
3. Type the product **Name**.
4. Optionally type a **SKU**. It must be unique within your organisation.
5. Choose the **Status**: **Active** or **Inactive**.
6. Enter the **Unit price** and check the **Currency**.
7. Optionally enter **Tax rate (%)** and a **Tax label** such as GST or VAT.
8. Leave the **Stock** switch on if the item can be sold now.
9. Optionally add a **Description**.
10. Click **Create product**.

## Result
"Product created" appears and the product is listed on the **Products** page.

## Tips
- Prices are per unit, before tax. An empty unit price is saved as 0.
- Only **Active** products can be picked on a deal.
- To add many products at once, use **Import products** on the empty page, or **Settings → Security & Data → Import & Export Data**.

## Troubleshooting
- **An error on SKU.** Another product already uses that SKU. Use a different one.
- **No Product button.** Your role cannot create products. Ask an Admin.
