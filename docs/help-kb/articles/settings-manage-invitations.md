## Overview
Pending invitations are listed at the top of **Users & Teams**. You can send a fresh link if the email was lost or expired, or revoke an invitation sent by mistake.

## Before you start
- Only the Owner and Admins can manage invitations.

## Steps
1. Click **Settings** in the left sidebar.
2. In the **Workspace** card, click **Users & Teams**.
3. Find the row with the status **Invited** or **Invite expired**.
4. Click the **…** actions button at the end of that row.
5. Click **Resend invitation** to send a new link, or click **Revoke invitation** to cancel it.
6. If you chose **Revoke invitation**, click **Revoke** to confirm.

## Result
- After resending: a message says **Invitation resent**. The previous link no longer works and the 7-day expiry starts again.
- After revoking: a message says **Invitation revoked** and the link stops working.

## Tips
- To invite the same person with a different role, revoke the invitation and send a new one.

## Troubleshooting
- **"Please wait a minute before resending."** Wait a minute and try again.
- **"This invitation has been sent too many times."** Revoke it and invite the person again.
