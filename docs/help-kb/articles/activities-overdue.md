## Overview
An activity is overdue when it is still open and its due or start time has passed. Overdue items are shown in red.

## Before you start
- You need `activities.view` (all built-in roles).

## Steps
1. Click **Activities** in the left sidebar.
2. Look at the **Overdue** card on the right side of the page. The number in brackets counts the overdue activities you can see.
3. Click an item in the card to open it, or click **View All** in the card.
4. In the list, open each item's **⋮** actions menu.
5. Click **Mark complete** if it is done, or **Reschedule** to give it a new date.

## Result
Completed or rescheduled items leave the overdue list, and the **Overdue** count goes down.

## Tips
- **My Day** has an **Overdue** section at the top.
- In the grouped list (click **Grouped list**), open the **Due** filter and choose **Overdue**. Rows are also grouped under an **Overdue** heading.
- The **Upcoming** card works the same way for the next seven days.
