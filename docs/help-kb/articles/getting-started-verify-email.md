## Overview
After you sign up, we send a verification link to your email. Opening it confirms the address and finishes setting up your workspace.

## Steps
1. Open the email we sent after you clicked **Create account**.
2. Click the verification link in the email.
3. Wait while the page shows **Verifying your email…**.
4. When you see **Email verified**, click **Open your CRM**. If you are not signed in, the button says **Sign in** instead.

## Result
The page says **Your workspace is ready.** and you can start using the CRM.

## Tips
- Each link works once. Opening it again later shows **Verification failed** even though your email is already verified; just sign in.

## Troubleshooting
- **"This link is invalid, has expired or was already used."** If you already verified your email, click **Go to sign in** and sign in. Otherwise create your account again to get a new link.
- **I cannot find the email.** Check your spam or promotions folder.
- **Sign in shows "Verify your email".** Your address is not verified yet. Open the link first, then sign in again.
