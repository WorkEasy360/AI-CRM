## Overview
Two-factor authentication asks for a 6-digit code from an authenticator app (such as Google Authenticator, 1Password or Aegis) each time you sign in, so a stolen password alone is not enough.

## Before you start
- Install an authenticator app on your phone.

## Steps
1. Click **Settings** in the left sidebar.
2. In the **Security & Data** card, click **Password & 2FA**.
3. In the **Two-factor authentication** card, click **Set up authenticator app**.
4. If a **Confirm it's you** window appears, enter your password and click **Confirm**.
5. In your authenticator app, add a new account and scan the QR code (or type the **Secret** shown).
6. Enter the 6-digit code from the app in **Confirmation code**.
7. Click **Enable two-factor**.

## Result
A message says **Two-factor authentication enabled**, the card shows **Enabled**, and your recovery codes appear. Save them somewhere safe.

## Tips
- To turn it off later, click **Disable two-factor**, then **Disable**. If your organization requires two-factor authentication, you lose access until you turn it on again.
- If you sign in with a Workeasy360 account, turn on two-step verification in Workeasy360. That is what your organization's requirement checks.

## Troubleshooting
- **"That code was not accepted."** Check that your phone's time is set automatically, then enter the newest code.
