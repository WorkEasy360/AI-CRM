## Overview
WorkEasy is built so that your workspace's data is visible only to your own team, and only to the extent each person's role allows.

## Steps
1. Protect your own account: open **Settings → Security & Data → Password & 2FA** and click **Set up authenticator app**.

## Result
Your account needs both your password and a code from your phone to sign in.

## Tips
- **Separate workspaces.** Every workspace's data is kept apart and checked at the database level on every request, so one company can never see another company's records.
- **Role-based access.** What each member sees and changes is checked by the server on every request, not just hidden in the screen.
- **Extra confirmation.** Sensitive changes, such as role changes or removing a user, ask you to confirm with your password.
- **Encrypted connections.** The app is used over a secure (HTTPS) connection, and passwords and keys for connected apps are stored encrypted.
- **Audit trail.** Owners and Admins can review security-relevant changes in the **Activity / Audit Log**.
- **Nothing on your device.** The app does not keep CRM records on your phone or computer for offline use.
- **Two-factor for everyone.** Owners and Admins can require two-factor authentication for all members.

## Troubleshooting
- **I think someone else used my account.** Change your password, then click **Sign out other sessions** under **Active sessions**, and tell your Owner or Admin.
