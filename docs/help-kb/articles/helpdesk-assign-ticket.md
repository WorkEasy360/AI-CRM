## Overview
Every ticket has one agent in **Assigned To**. A new ticket is assigned to the person who created it. Reassign it to move it to another agent's queue.

## Before you start
- You need `tickets.update` with access to all tickets to assign them to someone else. Owner, Admin and Sales Manager can do this. A Sales Representative can work on their own tickets but cannot hand them to another member.

## Steps
1. Click **Helpdesk** in the left sidebar.
2. Click the ticket's ID or subject in the list.
3. In the **Ticket Details** panel, click **More**.
4. Click **Reassign**.
5. Choose the agent in **Assign to**.
6. Click **Reassign**.

## Result
**Assigned To** shows the new agent, and the ticket appears in their queue straight away. The change is recorded in the ticket's **Activity** tab.

## Tips
- Use the **All Agents** filter, or choose **Me**, to see who has which tickets.
- The **Assigned To** column in the list shows **Unassigned** when no agent is set.

## Troubleshooting
- **"You cannot assign records to other members."** Your role can only keep tickets on yourself. Ask a Sales Manager or Admin to reassign it.
