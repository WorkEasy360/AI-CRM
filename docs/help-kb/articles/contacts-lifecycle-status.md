## Overview
Each contact and company has a status that shows where it is in your sales cycle: **Lead**, **Prospect**, **Qualified**, **Customer** or **Inactive**. New contacts start as Lead.

## Before you start
- You need to be able to edit the record. Sales Representatives can change records they own.

## Steps
1. Click **Contacts** in the left sidebar, then **People (Contacts)**.
2. Click the person's name to open the contact.
3. Click the status badge under the name (for example **Lead**).
4. Choose the new status from the **Status** menu.

## Result
A "Status updated" message appears and the badge shows the new status. The change is recorded on the **Timeline** tab.

## Tips
- On a company page, the status badge is in the row of facts under the company name.
- You can also change **Status** in the **Edit** form.
- Status affects the lead score: Customer and Qualified raise it, Inactive lowers it.

## Troubleshooting
- **The badge is not clickable?** You cannot edit this record.
- **"This record was changed by someone else"?** The latest version was loaded; choose the status again.
