## Overview
Suspending blocks a person's access immediately but keeps their role and teams, so you can bring them back later. **Revoke sessions** only signs them out of every device, for example after a lost laptop.

## Before you start
- Only the Owner and Admins can do this. Only the Owner can suspend another Owner.
- You cannot suspend yourself.

## Steps
1. Click **Settings** in the left sidebar.
2. In the **Workspace** card, click **Users & Teams**.
3. Click the **…** actions button at the end of the person's row.
4. Click **Suspend**.
5. In **Suspend user?**, click **Suspend**.
6. If a **Confirm it's you** window appears, enter your password and click **Confirm**.

## Result
A message says **User suspended** and the status changes to **Suspended**. The person is signed out everywhere and cannot use the organization.

## Tips
- To restore access, open the same menu, click **Reactivate**, then click **Reactivate** again to confirm. They return with their previous role and teams.
- To sign someone out without blocking them, click **Revoke sessions**, then **Revoke sessions** in **Sign out everywhere?**. They can sign in again.

## Troubleshooting
- **I want them gone for good.** Use **Remove user** instead (see the related article).
