## Overview
Keep a company's name, website, phone, industry, size and revenue up to date.

## Before you start
- Sales Representatives can edit companies they own. Owner, Admin and Sales Manager can edit all companies.

## Steps
1. Click **Contacts** in the left sidebar, then **Companies**.
2. Click the company's name to open it.
3. Click **Edit** at the top right.
4. Change **Name**, **Website**, **Phone**, **Industry** or **Status** as needed.
5. Click **More details** to change **Company size**, **Source**, **Annual revenue**, **Revenue currency**, **Description**, address or custom fields.
6. Click **Save changes**.

## Result
A "Company updated" message appears and the company page shows the new details.

## Tips
- You can also click the ⋮ button on a row in the Companies list and choose **Edit**.
- **Revenue currency** is a 3-letter code such as INR or USD; leave it empty to use your workspace currency.

## Troubleshooting
- **"Company name is required."** The Name field cannot be empty.
- **"This record was changed by someone else"?** Reload the page and make your change again.
