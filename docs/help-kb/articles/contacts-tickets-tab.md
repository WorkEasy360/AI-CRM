## Overview
The **Tickets** tab on a contact lists the support requests raised by that person, newest activity first, so you can check open issues before you call.

## Before you start
- You need permission to view tickets. Creating one needs ticket create permission (Owner, Admin, Sales Manager, Sales Representative).

## Steps
1. Click **Contacts** in the left sidebar, then **People (Contacts)**.
2. Click the person's name to open the contact.
3. Click the **Tickets** tab.
4. Click a ticket to open it in the Helpdesk.
5. To raise a new one, click **Create Ticket**.

## Result
Each ticket shows its number, subject, priority, status and when it last changed. **Create Ticket** opens the Helpdesk with the new ticket form and this contact already filled in.

## Tips
- The Tickets tab is on contact pages only.

## Troubleshooting
- **No Tickets tab?** Your role cannot view tickets.
- **"No tickets"?** This person has not raised any support requests yet.
