## Overview
Add a person (an individual) you work with so you can track calls, deals, emails and WhatsApp messages with them in one place.

## Before you start
- You need permission to create contacts. Owner, Admin, Sales Manager and Sales Representative can; Viewer cannot.

## Steps
1. Click **Contacts** in the left sidebar, then **People (Contacts)**.
2. Click **Add Contact** at the right of the tab bar.
3. Choose **Individual**.
4. Enter the **Full Name**.
5. Optionally, in **Company Name (Optional)**, search for a company and select it.
6. Pick the country code and enter the **Phone** number.
7. Optionally fill in **Email**, **Job Title / Designation**, **Tags** and **Notes**.
8. Click **Save Contact**.

## Result
The panel closes, a "Contact saved" message appears and the person shows in **All Contacts** and **Individuals**.

## Tips
- Full Name and Phone are required here. The first word of the name becomes the first name and the rest the last name.
- On the **Individuals** tab you can also click **Contact** for the longer form with Status, Owner, address and custom fields.
- To add many contacts at once, open **Add Contact** → **Import**, which takes you to Settings → Import & Export Data.

## Troubleshooting
- **No Add Contact button?** Your role cannot create contacts. Ask an Admin.
- **"Enter a valid phone number (7 to 15 digits)."** Check the number and the country code.
