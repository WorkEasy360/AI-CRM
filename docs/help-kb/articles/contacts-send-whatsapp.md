## Overview
Message a contact on WhatsApp from their record. Messages and replies are kept on the contact's **WhatsApp** tab.

## Before you start
- An Admin must connect the WhatsApp Business account in Settings → Communication → WhatsApp Integration.
- The contact needs a phone number.
- Owner, Admin, Sales Manager and Sales Representative can send.

## Steps
1. Click **Contacts** in the left sidebar, then **People (Contacts)**.
2. Click the person's name to open the contact.
3. Click **WhatsApp** in the quick actions under the contact's details.
4. If the customer wrote in the last 24 hours, type your text in **Message** and click **Send**.
5. Otherwise, choose a **Template**, fill in each value, and click **Send template**.

## Result
A "WhatsApp message queued" message appears and the message shows on the **WhatsApp** tab with its delivery ticks.

## Tips
- WhatsApp allows free text only within 24 hours of the customer's last message. Outside that window you can send only templates approved by Meta.
- Templates can be sent only to contacts who agreed to receive WhatsApp messages. Record consent with **Edit** → **More details** → **Contact agreed to receive WhatsApp messages**.
- In free-text mode, **Draft with AI** writes a draft for you to review.

## Troubleshooting
- **"WhatsApp is not connected for this workspace"?** Ask an Admin to connect it.
- **No WhatsApp button?** Add a phone number to the contact.
