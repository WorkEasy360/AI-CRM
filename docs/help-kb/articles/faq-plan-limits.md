## Overview
Each plan includes limits, for example the number of records, team pipelines, automation rules, custom fields per module and monthly AI credits. Users are limited to the number of licences on paid plans.

## Steps
1. When an action is blocked by your plan, read the window that opens (**You've reached your plan limit** or **Upgrade to** a plan) and click **Compare plans** or **View add-ons**, or click **Not now** to close it.

## Result
Nothing you already have is deleted or changed. Only the action that would go over the limit is stopped, and the window tells you what is used against the limit.

## Tips
- Plan limits at a glance:
  - **Free**: 1 user, 500 records, 1 pipeline, 3 automation rules, no custom fields, no AI.
  - **Express**: 50,000 records, 3 pipelines, 30 automation rules, 10 custom fields per module.
  - **Premier**: 100,000 records, 5 pipelines, 50 automation rules, 25 custom fields per module, 1,000 AI credits a month.
  - **360**: 1,000,000 records, 15 pipelines, 100 automation rules, 50 custom fields per module, 3,000 AI credits a month.
- An add-on raises one limit without changing plan (paid plans only).
- The **Usage** meters on **Plan & usage** warn you before you hit a limit.

## Troubleshooting
- **"Your plan's user limit has been reached."** Remove a user, or have the Owner add licences.
- **The AI assistant stops writing answers.** Check **AI credits** on **Plan & usage**. If your workspace's monthly AI budget is reached, Ask WorkEasy keeps answering from your CRM records only, until next month.
