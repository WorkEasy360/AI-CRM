## Overview
The **?** icon in the top bar is the **Help** menu. It gives quick ways into search, the AI assistant and Settings.

## Steps
1. Click the **?** icon (**Help**) in the top bar, between the bell and your avatar.
2. Choose an option:
   - **Search everything** opens global search (shortcut **Ctrl K**).
   - **Ask WorkEasy AI** opens the assistant panel on the right.
   - **Settings** opens the Settings home page.

## Result
The chosen tool opens without leaving the page you are on (Settings opens its own page).

## Tips
- **Ask WorkEasy AI** only opens the panel if your role can use the AI Assistant.
- The **Settings** item is a shortcut; **Settings** is also at the bottom of the left sidebar.
