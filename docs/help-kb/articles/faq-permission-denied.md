## Overview
Every action in WorkEasy is checked against your role. If your role does not allow it, you see a message such as "You do not have permission to perform this action." or a **No access** page, or the button simply does not appear.

## Steps
1. Ask your workspace Owner or an Admin to check your role on **Settings → Workspace → Users & Teams** and, if needed, change it or adjust your profile on **Roles & Permissions**.

## Result
Once your role includes the permission, sign in again if asked; the action then works.

## Tips
- Common causes:
  - **Sales Representative** can edit, delete or move only records they own, and sees only their team's records.
  - **Viewer** is read-only.
  - Users, roles, the audit log and most settings are limited to the Owner and Admins.
  - Billing changes are limited to the Owner.
- A field can be missing because a field permission hides it for your role; a record can be missing because it belongs to someone outside your team.
- When an Admin changes your role, you are signed out and get the new access when you sign in again.

## Troubleshooting
- **"Multi-factor authentication is required by your organization."** Set up two-factor authentication under **Password & 2FA**.
- **The upgrade window appears instead.** Your plan, not your role, blocks the action. See the plan limits FAQ.
