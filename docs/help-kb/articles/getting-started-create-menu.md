## Overview
The green **Create** button in the top bar lets you add a new record without leaving the page you are on.

## Before you start
- The menu only lists what your role may create. For example, a Viewer cannot create records, so the button is hidden.

## Steps
1. Click **Create** in the top bar (on narrow screens it shows only a **+** icon).
2. Under **Create**, choose one of **Contact**, **Company**, **Deal**, **Task**, **Call**, **Meeting** or **Ticket**.
3. Fill in the form that opens.
4. Save the form.

## Result
- **Contact**, **Company** and **Deal** open the new record's page after you save.
- **Task**, **Call** and **Meeting** are saved and the dialog closes; you stay on the same page.
- **Ticket** takes you to the **Helpdesk** with the new ticket panel open.

## Tips
- On a phone, tap **New** in the bottom bar. It offers **Task**, **Call**, **Contact** and **Deal**.
- The Dashboard **Quick Actions** panel has the same shortcuts for contacts, deals, tasks and meetings.

## Troubleshooting
- **"Could not load pipelines" when creating a deal.** A deal needs a pipeline stage. Reload the page; if it persists, ask an administrator to check your pipelines.
