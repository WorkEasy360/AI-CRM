## Overview
Edit a tag to change its name or colour everywhere it is used. Delete a tag to remove it from every record that carries it.

## Before you start
- You need the Owner, Admin or Sales Manager role (`tags.manage`).

## Steps
1. Click **Settings** in the left sidebar.
2. In the **CRM Configuration** card, click **Tags**.
3. Click the **⋯** button at the end of the tag's row (labelled **Actions for** and the tag name).
4. To rename or recolour it, click **Edit**.
5. Change the **Name** or **Colour**, then click **Save changes**.
6. To remove it instead, click **Delete** in the same menu, then click **Delete tag** to confirm.

## Result
You see "Tag updated" or "Tag deleted". A renamed tag changes on every record; a deleted tag disappears from the list and from all records.

## Tips
- Check the **Usage** column before deleting: the confirmation also tells you how many records will lose the tag.

## Troubleshooting
- **Deleted the wrong tag?** Deleting cannot be undone. Create the tag again and re-apply it to the records.
