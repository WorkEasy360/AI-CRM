## Overview
The **AI Quick Actions** panel on the AI Assistant page starts common jobs in one click: some ask the assistant for a draft or summary, others open a form or page.

## Steps
1. Click **AI Assistant** in the left sidebar.
2. Find **AI Quick Actions** on the right.
3. Click a tile:
   - **Write Message** **(WhatsApp / Email)** drafts a short follow-up message for your newest lead.
   - **Create Automation** opens **Settings → Automation** (needs automation access).
   - **Create Task** opens the new task form.
   - **Schedule Meeting** opens the new meeting form.
   - **Generate Proposal** drafts a proposal outline for your largest open deal.
   - **Summarize Lead** summarizes your newest lead and what to do next.
   - **Get Reports** opens the Pipeline **Analytics** tab (needs deal access).
4. For more ideas, click **More Tools** and choose **Create follow-up sequence**, **Suggest next action**, **Create WhatsApp campaign**, **Analyze pipeline** or **Automation ideas**.

## Result
Prompts appear in the conversation with the assistant's answer. Form tiles open the create dialog on the same page.

## Tips
- Drafts are text for you to copy and send yourself. The assistant does not send messages.
- Tiles are disabled while the assistant is still answering.
