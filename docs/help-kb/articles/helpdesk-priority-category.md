## Overview
Priority (**Low**, **Medium**, **High**, **Urgent**) decides how quickly a ticket must be resolved under the SLA. Category (**Technical**, **Billing**, **Account**, **Support**, **Sales**, **Other**) groups tickets by topic.

## Before you start
- You need `tickets.update` for the ticket. Owner, Admin and Sales Manager can change any ticket; a Sales Representative can change tickets assigned to them.

## Steps
1. Click **Helpdesk** in the left sidebar.
2. Click the ticket's ID or subject to open **Ticket Details**.
3. To change the category, open the **Category** list and pick a new one.
4. To change the priority, open the **Priority** list and pick a new one.

## Result
The change saves straight away and the list's **Category** and **Priority** columns update. A new priority also changes the ticket's **Due (SLA)** time.

## Tips
- You can also click **More** and pick a priority under **Change priority**.
- Raising a ticket to **Urgent** gives it the shortest SLA, so it may become **Overdue** sooner.

## Troubleshooting
- **The lists are read-only?** Your role can view this ticket but not change it.
