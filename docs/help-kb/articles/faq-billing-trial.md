## Overview
Answers to common questions about trials, plans and who can change billing.

## Steps
1. Open **Settings**, click **Billing & Subscription** next to **Also in Settings:** and check the **Current plan** card for your plan, its status and renewal date.

## Result
The badge next to your plan name tells you where you stand: **Trial** with the days left, **Active**, **Payment due**, **Trial ended** or **Legacy · full access**.

## Tips
- **What does the trial include?** New workspaces can start with a 15-day trial of the Premier plan.
- **What happens when it ends?** The workspace moves to the Free plan limits (1 user, 500 records). Your data stays; actions beyond the Free limits are blocked until the Owner chooses a paid plan.
- **Who can change the plan?** Only the Owner. Admins can view the page.
- **How is it priced?** Per user per month. Prices for **Monthly** and **Annual (save up to 22%)** are shown on each plan card; the number of **Licences** sets how many users you can have.
- **Legacy · full access** means your workspace has the full product without plan limits.

## Troubleshooting
- **"Plan changes for this workspace are handled by our billing team."** Plan changes cannot be made in the app for your workspace. Contact support.
- **I need invoices or to update a payment card.** These are not available in the app. Contact support.
